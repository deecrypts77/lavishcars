## Understand How Weekend Rates Work for Bentley Rentals

The easiest way to save money on a Bentley Continental GT in Dubai is to take advantage of weekend discounts. Because car rental companies typically charge higher rates for one-day rentals, booking for the weekend or long term may result in major savings and special deals and offers.

### Weekend Special Offers

Most car rental companies provide special weekend discounts on cars from Thursday to Sunday, allowing customers to rent for additional days without paying additional charges.

### Off-Peak Periods

Most car rental companies are busier during the working days than on weekends, which is one of the reasons why weekend bookings can be discounted.

### Flat Rates for Longer Rentals

When you rent a Bentley for a long duration of time, such as two or three days, the rental companies normally provide flat rates, making weekend renting typically less expensive than renting for a shorter period of time.

So in this way, you can enjoy a weekend booking of a Bentley Continental GT in a budget-friendly way without compromising on quality.
