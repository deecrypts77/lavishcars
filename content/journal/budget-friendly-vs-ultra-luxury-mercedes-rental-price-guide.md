## What Do “Budget-Friendly” and “Ultra-Luxury” Meanw to Mercedes Rentals?

### Renting of Mercedes at a low cost:

A car that is low budget friendly is not a second hand battered car. It is a luxurious, comfortable, and prestigious Mercedes that does not cost as much as a supercar or a limited-edition exotic. Consider the models such as the [C-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet), [E-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet), GLC or even the A-Class or GLB. These offer cars that provide you with a luxurious feel and are affordable.

Key characteristics:

- The lower luxury daily rental rate.
- Fine interiors, high quality badge, business or leisure.
- Mileage charges and extra charges can be extra.
- Suited to daily luxury driving, urban use, airport transfers or hotel pickups.

### Ultra- Luxury Mercedes Rental

Ultra-luxury refers to those that are well above a good sedan and into full-flagship, including S-Class four-wheelbase, [Maybach](/uae/dubai#fleet), [AMG GT](/uae/dubai#fleet), G63 or Brabus-tuned models, etc. Such rentals serve VIPs, weddings, upscale business events, photo shoots or just showcasing heads.

Key characteristics:

- Exorbitant daily charge, weekly or monthly premiums.
- Premium quality specifications: greater power, greater interior luxury, greater exclusivity.
- Can be accompanied by concierge or airport service; a chauffeur can be offered.
- Long luxury road trips, high profile events or statements.

## Things to Watch Out for and Things That Affect Prices

Some of the considerations that will increase or enable saving of the rented car will include when you rent a Mercedes. Here are the key levers.

**1.Model & Variant**

- An average Mercedes C-Class or A- Class is significantly less expensive than an AMG model or Maybach.  
- Luxury versions (leather, rear-seat entertainment, long wheelbase), are more expensive.  
- The SUVs tend to be expensive compared to sedans because of the size, space, and branding (e.g., GLS vs S-Class).

**2.Model Year & Condition**

- Newer model year = higher cost.  
- Cost can be increased by mileage, extras (carbon fibre trims, high-end sound systems etc.).  
- There are special limited edition or tuner models of some rentals (e.g., Brabus), increasing the price.

**3.Rental Duration**

- The daily rate is the highest per day.  
- Per-day cost is reduced by weekly rentals (10-20% off or better).  
- Monthly rentals can be the most attractive (e.g., Lavish Cars monthly with GLS: AED 102,000 per month on the first 30 days 30 days will cost AED 3,400/day on average).

**4.Mileage, Delivery and Inclusions**

- It may have many rentals which come with standard mileage – e.g. Lavish Cars states 250km/day included.  
- Toll/Salik charges, fuel policy can be expensive, over-km charges.  
- The airport delivery/hotel drop-off fees can be provided. Others are provided separately; others are provided with it. To illustrate, one of the websites offers a fee of AED 185- 235 to deliver to the airport.

**5.Season & Demand**

- The high rental demand (Nov -Mar) is influenced by peak seasons of the tourists – a higher rate is likely.  
- Weddings in Dubai, events or exhibitions can push the price up or decrease the availability.  
- There can be discounts during off season (summer), long term rental.

**6.Extras & Service Level**

- Chauffeur, concierge pick/drop and custom branding (weddings) are an additional cost.  
- The services are customized or made photos shootable, which increases cost.  
- The luxury cars can be accompanied by the high-quality service (meet and greet, VIP lounge).

**7.Hidden Costs&#xA0;**

Ensure that the car has a full tank or that you are familiar with the fuel policy. Lavish Cars usually provides a full tank.  

- Toll fees: There may be Salik or other road taxes; be sure to check.  
- Deposit/hold: This is where rentals will require a large security deposit (cash or card hold) or excess insurance.  
- Mileage: The amount of excess km/day can be costly.  
- Damage/cleaning: There are some luxury cars that have hard policies.  
- Age/license: You must be 25 years old or have 3 years of license experience, particularly with the premium models.
