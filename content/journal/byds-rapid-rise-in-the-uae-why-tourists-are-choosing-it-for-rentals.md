## The Rise of a Game-Changer in Luxury Electric Rentals

Only five years ago, BYD was a name very few passengers arriving at Dubai International Airport knew. For years later, the Chinese electric vehicle giant now competes to be one of the most popular names in the UAE car-renting market. Particularly among those foreigners who seek a less wasteful, more sustainable mode of exploration.

BYD is quickly becoming the byword of luxury EVs, although names such as Tesla and BMW continue to dominate the dialogue. The reason? An affordable, stylish, sustainable, and innovative model, all packaged together in a car that surprisingly fits on the UAE roads.

This post will take a closer look at why visitors in Dubai and the UAE are deciding to use BYD more as their rental car and how this electric brand is transforming what it means to drive luxury during a vacation.

## Why Tourists Are Flocking to BYD Rentals in the UAE

### 1. Smart Luxury Without the High Price Tag

The truth is, Dubai certainly has a reputation of being a place of luxury, but people are also more economical in their spending these days. It is where BYD enters. BYD features luxurious designs, which compete with European premium automobiles, and advanced technology features within, creating a luxurious vibe at a packed price that is extremely affordable.

Consider the [BYD Seal](/uae/dubai#fleet). It boasts a modernistic interior, fast-charging options, and a silent ride, on par with the Tesla Model 3, yet it is 20-30 percent less expensive per day as a rental option.

### 2. Eco-Conscious Travel Is Now Cool

Tourists are now becoming carbon-aware as never before. A significant number of passengers who attend the UAE still desire to indulge, though they also wish to travel in a more sustainable manner. The fully electric lineup of BYD perfectly fits into that requirement.

One of the latest models in this segment, the BYD Tang, in particular, hits the mark for those who prefer family trips and like a green vehicle that offers large seating capacity. It will be an ideal combination when cruising the Dubai coastlineor making a road trip to Abu Dhabi or Fujairah without adding the guilt of high emissions.

### 3. Advanced Tech That Impresses

When tourists pay to rent cars, it is often a car that they feel is newer and more advanced than their own. BYD does not disappoint. The experience is supremely futuristic with rotating infotainment displays, AI-enabled climate control, and a cutting-edge ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems).

A German tourist who was traveling by himself recently [hired the BYD via Lavish Cars](/uae/dubai#fleet), and his experience with the technology was, as he described, that it was a future gadget. They were especially impressed with the smart adaptive cruise control that managed [Sheikh Zayed Road](https://www.bayut.com/area-guides/sheikh-zayed-road/) with its smooth ease.

## Helpful Tips for First-Time BYD Renters in the UAE

- Make a reservation: BYD rentals are getting increasingly popular, particularly in the tourist season.
- Request a charging card: some of the rental companies provide a pre-loaded EV charging card.
- Plan your journey and the charging stops: Even apps like PlugShare will allow you to find charging stations throughout the UAE.
- Book midweek: It is usually cheaper Monday through Thursday.
- Pick up some slack: You may reserve a Song, but by the end of the first day you may love a Seal.
