Some people think that a car rental security deposit is some sort of extra fee or charges, its not. It is just temporary money that is held by rental companies until they are sure that you returned the car safely and are satisfied that everything is clear. A big amount being blocked on your credit card might stress you out especially when you are [**renting a car in dubai**](/uae/dubai) for the first time. This is more common if you choose renting a luxury or a sports car. There would be alot questions popping up in your mind, but its not really something to be worried about and It’s also okay to ask when you will receive it back. 

Security deposits follow a simple process. Understanding how they work helps you to stay confident the entire trip. Here you will find out why a car rental security deposit is required, how credit card holds operate, why the wait time for refunds varies by customer, and how to prevent delays. We’ll also discuss the impact of Dubai traffic fines, Salik charges, and banks on rental deposit returns.  

## How long does a security deposit refund take in Dubai? 

**The security deposit usually takes between 15 and 30 days to become available again. This will depend on your bank, the payment method used, and pending charges.**

Most people expect the refund to happen the same day they return the car. That rarely happens. The rental company first checks a few things after you return the vehicle. They ensure that the vehicle is in a good condition. They also see if there are any traffic tickets, Salik tickets, or parking tickets issued. 

One everything is inspected, the company start the release process. Then the bank does its work. This is the reason why sometimes, two people renting the same car may get paid on different days.

Here are some common reasons for timeline changes:

**Credit cards are usually faster**

When the security deposit Dubai is given as a credit card hold, the funds may never have been withdrawn from your account. Rather, the bank just took a portion of your total credit limit. As soon as the rental company removes the hold, your account balance is adjusted accordingly. This is what many banks do within a few business days. Some take longer. 

**Debit cards may take more time** 

The charge on debit card works differently. The funds can be removed from your account first. It takes time for your bank to process the refund after the company approves it. As a result, there may be a delay in debit card refunds compared to credit card holds.

**International banks may add extra waiting time&#xA0;**

Many tourists use cards from banks outside the UAE. International banks have their own processing systems. There are banks that will release holds rapidly. Others may require additional business days. This is where two travelers from different countries could possibly have different rental refund timelines.

**Weekends and holidays also matter&#xA0;**

Not all banks operate on a daily basis. As for the refund, if it begins before a weekend or a public holiday, then it can remain pending, until banks reopen. This delay does not indicate a problem. It often means that the banking system hasn’t finished the process yet.

## What is the difference between credit card pre-authorization and debit charges? 

**A credit card pre-authorization is a temporary authorization that takes away some amount of your credit limit while a debit charge is permanent and removes money from your bank account.&#xA0;**

### What is a credit card pre-authorization? 

A pre-authorization is just a short term bankhold. The rental company asks your bank to reserve a certain amount as your car rental security deposit. This money is not to be spent. It is only kept aside while you have the car. Its like lets say you made a booking for a seat at the cinema. The seat is reserved for you so no one is sitting in it.

It works the same way for your card. The bank holds a portion of your available credit back till the end of the rental period. If everything goes ok, the hold is lifted and your available credit goes back to normal.

**Why does it look like money has disappeared?&#xA0;**

This is one of the most common questions rental companies get to hear almost every day. You log into your banking app and see that available balance or credit limit has decreased. It is easy to believe that your renting company has already used your money. That is not usually what happened. Your bank is just letting you know that they’re holding those money for the duration of renting. They reappear upon the release of the hold. 

### How does a debit card work? 

A debit card can be different. The bank can simply subtract the amount from your account without a hold on your spending limit. At the end of your rent, the company begins the refund procedure. Your bank then sends the money back once it’s done its due diligence. This additional process makes many people realize that they are receiving their debit card refunds later than their credit card holds.

**Which payment method is better?**

A lot of the rental firms like credit cards, since pre authorizations are simpler to handle . Credit cards also mean that, for most of their customers, the funds remain in their account rather than being entirely removed.

It doesn’t mean that debit card is a bad choice. They just function a little differently. When you are checking out a rental company, make sure to inquire what payment methods they accept before booking. If you will be using a debit card, it is also important to ask your bank how long their refunds typically take.

### RTA traffic fine clearance windows & banking delays

Your deposit is not always returned to you the same day you bring the car back. Rental companies first wait for any pending road charges to appear before closing your booking. It’s often a confusing part for first time renters. You return the car, everything looks perfect. It doesn’t have any scratches. Fuel level is correct. You give the keys and you think your deposit should be returned to you immediately.

Why does company want you to wait, then? The answer is simple. All road charges are not fast. There are certain Dubai traffic fines that take a while to appear in the official system. It can also occur with Salik toll records and paid parking records.

For instance, if you are drive through one of the Salik gates on your way back. The charge might not show up right away. It makes it much more difficult for you to recover any unpaid amount after your deposit has been released by the rental company.

So companies tend to wait until these records are updated. This is good for the customer and the rental company. When you’re not sure you’ll receive a refund, the safest thing to do is to contact the company first. They can confirm if the hold has already been released. If it does, your bank will be able to tell you when the money will be available. You can also find up to date traffic rules and the fines information from the official site of [Dubai RTA](http://rta.ae/).

## How can you avoid unfair deposit deductions when returning a supercar? 

**Take clear, pictures and videos of the supercar and check it with the rental company before returning it to prevent unexpected deposit deductions.**

Take a few minutes before and after you use the car to look for details. It is important to keep good records and adhere to the rental agreement to prevent accidental charges against your security deposit. Luxury and supercars receive a careful inspection before every rental. It’s the same when you return them. This is an expected part of the rental agreement. This is useful to make sure you and the rental company both know the car is in the same state.

Here are some basic routines that can help the return much easier:

**Inspect the car before you drive away&#xA0;**

Inspect the car before you drive away. Do not rush when leaving the parking area. Explore the car by walking around. Examine the front bumper, doors, mirrors, wheels and rear bumper. Then check the inside of the car. Let the rental staff member know immediately if you notice a scratch, a dent, or a stain. Ask them to add it to the inspection report before you start your trip. This helps you from being held responsible for damages that already exist.

**Take photos and a short video**

Your cell phone can be your very best proof. Get clear photographs of front, back, sides, top, wheels and interior of the vehicle. A quick walk around video can be useful.

Do the same when you return the car. The majority of people do not need these shots. If at any time there is doubt about the car’s condition they can clear it up quickly.

**Return the car at the agreed time**

Late fees might be applied if the car is returned late. If your plans change, call the rental company as soon as possible. They will help you anyway. A short phone call is far better than being late and not informing anyone.

**Check the fuel level**

Always view rental agreements. Some companies require you to give the car back in the same condition as you picked it up. If it was picked up with full tank, then give full tank on return. This small step could help you avoid refuel charges.

**Pay attention to parking tickets**

Parking tickets get lost in a hurry, especially during your vacation. If you get one on your trip, notify the rental company. It is best to be honest from the start so the process will run a lot smoother.

**Avoid smoking inside the vehicle**

Most luxury cars for rent are non smoking. Smoke smell remains in the car for longer periods. Professional cleaning is usually needed to remove the smell. If you follow this advice you will help to ensure that the next customer can enjoy a fresh car, and you will avoid the risk of losing your security deposit.

**Drive carefully around curbs**

A lot of people are concerned about the scratches on the body when they rent their first luxury car. In fact, damage to the wheels is also common. Parking too close to a curb can leave the alloy wheels marked, which can cost a big amount of money.

**Keep the interior clean**

Luxury cars offer high quality leather, soft materials and finishings. Avoid messy foods in vehicle. Wipe up any spills immediately. Keeping the car clean shows respect to the vehicle and save you from any cleaning charges.

**Ask for a final inspection** 

Before leaving the rental office, ask if someone can inspect the car with you. This takes only a few minutes, and will prevent potential miscommunications later on. The process is similar at all the pick up points, including Downtown Dubai, where many tourists pick up and drop off rentals.
