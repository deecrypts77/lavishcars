## A Brief History: How the G-Class Became a Global Icon

Mercedes G-Class was introduced in the late 1970s as an off-road car of military standard. It was almost impossible to stop on any ground due to its boxy shape, ladder frame chassis, and three locking differentials.

Throughout the decades, Mercedes made the G-Class a luxury SUV but still retained its DNA. It is one of the few cars that have not changed its iconic shape today but has developed into a high-performance high-prestige car.

The G-Class was a status symbol in Dubai. It is a tradition among the garages of royalties, celebrities, businessmen and women, and car lovers. To the locals and tourists, [renting a G-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet) would be a way of enjoying a portion of Dubai’s cultural brand – a brand that is power-driven, luxurious, and exclusive.

## Why the G-Class Is Dubai’s Ultimate Icon

There is hardly a car with as rich a legacy as the Mercedes G-Class. The G-Wagon, first tailored as a 4×4 in the 1970s as a military vehicle, has evolved into a now luxury powerhouse SUV despite retaining its unmistakable boxy shape. In Dubai where culture and technology merge, the G-Class will be at home.

### 1. Rugged Off-Road Capabilities

Dubai is not only high buildings and malls, it is also dunes and wadis and paths of the desert. The G-Class is built for this:

- **Three locking differentials:&#x20;**&#x63;ommon in few SUVs, which allowed maximum grip on dune climbing.
- **4MATIC all-wheel drive:&#x20;**&#x74;he distribution of power throughout the car is smart.
- **High ground clearance:** 9.5+ inches to overcome rocks, crests of the sand and irregular desert surface.
- **Wading depth:** 27 inch and is perfect when in the desert wadis or the little creeks.

These features will ensure that the G-Class will feel as though the deserts are meant to be driven through, whether going to a luxurious camp at Al Marmoom or before the sunsets by Al Qudra Lakes.

### 2. Powerful V8 Performance

Without performance, luxury is meaningless, and the G-Wagon does not fail in this regard:

- 4.0L twin-turbocharged V8 engine
- 577 horsepower (AMG G63 trim)
- 0–100 km/h in \~4.5 seconds

It is sports-car performance in a 2.5 ton off-road SUV. The screaming of the V8 attracts the notice of the people on the Sheikh Zayed Road. The torque is what keeps you going on the sand dunes. It is the all-purpose car they can use either in the city nightlife or the desert.

### 3. Prestige Factor: A Status Symbol in Dubai

Being honest, Dubai is more of a place to leave a mark, than an experience. The G-Wagon is attention grabbing.

- Arriving at the Burj Khalifa, Atlantis The Palm, or Bulgari Resort driving in G63 AMG immediately makes one one who appreciates luxury and tradition.
- The AMG styling, which is aggressive, and the boxy design is an exception in the world of SUVs.
