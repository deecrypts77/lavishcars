## Mercedes SUVs in Dubai—Spacious, Bold, and Adventurous Ready

### 1. Mercedes GLS: The “S-Class of SUVs”

The Mercedes GLS is not just any SUV, it is sometimes referred to as the S-Class of SUVs, and rightly so. This vehicle is a blend of sheer size and lavish comfort and therefore one of the most family-friendly luxury cars in Dubai.

- Why It Is Unique:Three seats per row combined with a sophisticated suspension and luxury interior have made it feel like a five-star crew riding on the wheels.
- Best For: Families, travelling in a group, or when you are bringing additional baggage on long-term vacations.
- Dubai Moments: Best when you want to go on a road trip to the Louvre Museum in Abu Dhabi or a weekend getaway to the mountains in [Ras Al Khaimah](https://www.tripreporter.co.uk/insider-guide-to-ras-al-khaimah/).

The experience of driving a GLS is similar to being in a first-class cabin, but the cabin happens to be on the road of Sheikh Zayed. It is not as flashy as the G-Wagon but is still elegant in a way that sophisticated travelers would like.

![Mercedes G63 AMG](/images/journal/2025-07-mercedes-g63-amg.webp)

### 2. Mercedes G-Class – The Icon of Power

There is hardly a car in the world that has the same atmosphere as the [Mercedes G-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet), or the G-Wagon. It is not only a car in Dubai but also a symbol of status.

- What is special about it: Unbelievable off-road capability with a bold and instantly recognizable look.
- Best for VIP flyers, couples who desire to be noticed, or any person who desires prestige.
- Dubai Moments: Dining at the Burj Al Arab, driving to a yacht night at Dubai Marina, or retreating into the desert to have fun.

G-Wagon provides the sensation of off-road power with the tranquility of leather seats, high-tech equipment, and an engine that sounds as loud as the Dubai club life.

## The Sleek Sedans: Mercedes E-Class & C-Class

### Mercedes E-Class – The Executive’s Companion

While the GLS is a moving lounge, the E-Class is a mobile executive suite. All of this sedan is specially created even to satisfy the requirements of the professional who seeks performance, comfort, and quiet power.

- Why It Is Special: High-end design, state-of-the-art driver aids, and the best cabin sound.
- Best for: Business travelers, corporate clients, or couples that would prefer discrete luxury.
- Dubai Moments: Airport transfer, meeting a client in DIFC, or a night out in Zuma or La Petite Maison.

The E-Class does not shout its head off, it commands through refinement. Consider it the perfect custom-made suit on four wheels.

### Mercedes C-Class – Compact Luxury with Sporty Soul

The Mercedes C-Class is the perfect compromise between driveability and luxury, which can be appreciated by those travelers who enjoy driving. It is smaller than the E-Class, but not without sporty DNA, it is designed to be enjoyed with luxury in a smaller frame.

- What is Special about it: Smooth design, turbocharging engines, and interior orientation towards drivers.
- Best: Solo travellers, couples or those who want high quality comfort at a reduced daily rate.
- Dubai Moments: Romantic cruises on Jumeirah Beach Road, fast shopping trips in the Mall of the Emirates, or going around Downtown at sunset.

The C-Class is personalized to every drive and it is intimate, stylish, and fun.

## Style Meets Function: Which Works Best in Dubai?

The SUV versus Sedan is not a matter of preference in Dubai but a matter of lifestyle.

- To Families: The GLS is undefeated in comfort, space, and convenience over a long distance.
- In the case of Statement-Makers: The G-Class is a deceiver, whether in a luxury hotel or in the desert drive.
- To the Executives: The E-Class provides the status of a professional with the flash that is not needed.
- C-Class: The C-Class offers a mix of style and affordability to the Couples or First-Timers.

The broad highways and clean city roads of Dubai make both types fun, though the choice of which one is correct is determined after you experience your own story.

## The Cost of Luxury: SUVs vs Sedans

Budget is a consideration when it comes to renting. SUVs are more expensive, as they are bigger and prestigious. Sedans are cheaper, but without luxury.

SUVs:

- Mercedes GLS: AED 3400–3500/day
- Mercedes G-Class: AED 7500–8000/day

Sedans:

- Mercedes E-Class: AED 600–900/day
- Mercedes C-Class: AED 800–900/day

💡 Trick: It usually costs less to book in the middle of the week, as weekends and events spur demand. Renting can also be taken in the long run, and this saves on daily expenses.
