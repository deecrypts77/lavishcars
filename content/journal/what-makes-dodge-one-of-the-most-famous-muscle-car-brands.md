The first name that comes to mind when you consider sheer power, violent design and the roar of a V8 engine is Dodge. Dodge has established a reputation to be more than a car maker since Hollywood films to the actual streets. It is an attitude, performance and a legacy that has deep roots in the American muscle car culture.

## The History of Dodge Muscle Cars

Dodge is an automotive company that was established in 1900 and has been in the business over a hundred years. Nevertheless, it actually gained its foothold in the golden age of the muscle cars of the 1960s and 70.

Such models as Dodge Charger and [**Dodge Challenger**](https://www.dodge.com/challenger.html) soon became the icons of speed and rebellion. Such cars were not just made to drive around to point B, but to conquer the road. Dodge still pays tribute to its history by making cars that combine the classic muscle cars with the modern performance technology even to date.

## What Defines a True Muscle Car?

There is a need to know what makes a muscle car. a muscle car before going further to the success of Dodge.

A real muscle car usually incorporates:

- An engine with a strong V8.
- Rear wheel drive
- Bold, aggressive styling
- Straight line speed performance

Dodge fits all these boxes, and usually fills them, too.

## Power That Speaks for Itself

Among the largest counts of reasons that Dodge can be singled out is its uninhibited emphasis on power. An example is the Dodge Challenger SRT Hellcat. With a power of more than 700 horsepower, this beast is one of the strongest production muscle cars of all times. Secondly, there is the Dodge Charger SRT Hellcat, a four door muscle car with the performance of a supercar and the practicality of a regular car.

Dodge has remained true to its heritage by remaining the only company in a world where most brands are reducing engine sizes to produce high displacement, high performance engines to delight their drivers.

## Bold and Aggressive Design

Dodge cars can not be skipped. Their broad bodies, sharp contours as well as their aggressive posture make them immediately noticeable on the road. As opposed to a lot of modern vehicles which strive to be sleek and minimalistic in their designs, Dodge has a loud and in-your face appearance. This is the design philosophy which most drivers, who desire their car to have a statement, find appealing.

The muscular hood scoops are designed to display might and control, as are the signature LED lighting, and everything combining to display a feeling of power.

## A Strong Cultural Impact

Popularity of Dodge is not only concerning performance but also culture. Films such as Fast and Furious have contributed immensely to the cementing of the Dodge image. The Dodge Charger R/T that is driven by Vin Diesel has become an icon overnight. Such exposure has transformed Dodge to be a favorite of the young people and car lovers across the globe. It is not a mere car, it is a way of life.

## Modern Innovation Meets Classic Muscle

Dodge has not forgotten about the future even as it glorifies its past. The current generation of Dodge vehicles is fitted with:

- Advanced infotainment systems
- Driver assistance technologies
- High performance braking systems
- Custom driving modes

Old school muscle and new technology combined make Dodge vehicles incredibly attractive, particularly among luxury car renters who need comfort and excitement.

As the automotive industry changes to electric cars, Dodge is changing as well.
