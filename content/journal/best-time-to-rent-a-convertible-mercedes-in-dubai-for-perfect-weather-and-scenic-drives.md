## Know the Right Season to Rent a Convertible Mercedes in Dubai

The climate in Dubai contributes immensely towards determining the time to drive with an open top. The city is sun savvy throughout the year, though not every month is convertible friendly.

Let’s break it down:

### Best Months of the Year to drive a convertible: November to March.

- It is the best season in Dubai, with cool evenings, moderate weather and a cool breeze over the sea.
- Daytime temps: 22°C–30°C
- Evening temps: 17°C–22°C

It is the perfect choice to drive the roof down and go cruising along the [Jumeirah Beach](https://www.jumeirah.com/en/stay/dubai/jumeirah-beach-hotel) Road or take a trip along the Palm Jumeirah during sunset. It is best to take an early morning or a sunset drive. You will capture golden light, traffic will be avoided, and those perfect shots will be made.

### Editor Season: April and October.

Nevertheless, great months still, slightly warmer, but it can be controlled when you drive on colder days. April and October would be ideal in case you prefer a lower rental rate (this is during the low or off season for tourists). During these months, convertible rentals are usually discounted up to 20 percent.

### Avoid: May to September (Summer Months)

Dubai is hot between May and September, as the temperature may go from 40°C to 48°C—not the kind of place to drive in an open-top car. Even upscale convertibles with high-tech climate control and ventilated seats cannot entirely struggle with the rays of the blazing sun. But in case you still desire that convertible feeling, take it out at night, the lights in the city, the fresher air, and less traffic justify the expense.

You can go with a [Mercedes E-Class Cabriolet](/uae/dubai#fleet) or Mercedes SL-Class: both have thermatic climate control, great level heating, and wind deflectors so that you can enjoy top-down drives even in marginally hot climates.

## Weatherproof Your Convertible Experience

The sun of Dubai is a monster even during the cooler days. Now here is the way to keep your cool with the top open:

1. Take the Mercedes Airscarf and Aircap, they will keep the cold out and the warmth around your neck. These whizz-bangs blow cool air around your neck and prevent the wind from playing around with you, perfect on a night drive.
2. Bring sunscreen and sunglasses due to the reason. Even in December a desert sun may be a mean one. Slather up, and wear your shades when you have your long drives.
3. Drink water like it’s your job. Have a bottle at hand. There is no ninja dehydration with that sun, where you will not see it, you will feel it.

4) Play smart with the roof. Those Convertibles put on the roof and take it off in a flash. Once the heat reaches above 32°C, stick it up and cool using the AC.
