## How do Salik toll tags work automatically in rental cars?

**Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology installed on the windscreen of rental cars is used to scan a Salik tag as you pass a toll gate without having to stop.**

Salik is the electronic toll system of Dubai. It employs a small tag attached to the car windscreen. The system reads the tag when the car passes a Salik gate, and saves the trip. According to the official website of Dubai tolls, all tolls are deducted automatically from the vehicle as it passes through a toll gate.

You can learn more about toll collection and applicable toll arrangements on the [official Salik website](https://www.salik.ae/en/about/how-it-works).

This also applies to car rental. The car already belongs to the rental company. The Salik system is typically mounted on that vehicle. During your rental, the company you rent from can then see the tolls associated with the car. If you rented a car, you will not normally be required to purchase another Salik tag. There also is no need to stop if you encounter a Salik gate.

Let’s say you rent a Lamborghini for three days. You are driving from Dubai Marina to Downtown Dubai and drive through Salik gate. The crossing is recorded on the gate. You keep driving. The rental company can claim or receive that Toll Charge later.

The exact manner of the charge is depends on the rental company. A few companies may tack on Salik charges at the end of your bill. There could be a different billing process somewhere else. There also may be a service or administration fee. This is why it’s important to be able to read the rental agreement before driving off.

### How much does Salik cost for a rental car?

Your charge depends on the time you pass through a toll gate of Salik. The Dubai Salik has been launched with a variable Salik pricing system from January 2025. The published structure of the weekdays currently suggests the ticket will be AED 6 at peak time and AED 4 at off-peak time. The toll is free from 1:00 am to 6:00 am. It costs AED 4 on Sundays except during the free early morning period. The peak hour of Ramadan can be different.

Currently published timings are:

- Peak: 6:00 am to 10:00 am and 4:00 pm to 8:00 pm on weekdays
- Off peak: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm and 8:00 pm to 1:00 am
- Past midnight: 1:00 am to 6:00 am
- Out of free early morning hours; Sunday: AED 4.

These rules and rates can change. Verify the current official information before your travel. If you are renting a car for a few days, and you go through two toll gates every day, you could find that you have multiple Salik charges by the end of the rental.

However, this does not mean that you are fined. It is just the fee for the toll road, there is no surcharge or extra charge involved. 

## What are the RTA speed camera grace limits on Dubai highways?

**Highways in Dubai typically impose an unofficial allowance limit or buffer of 20 km/h over the authorised speed limit in response to speed cameras (radars) giving a fine.** 

But this should not be considered as an additional speed allowance you are entitled to use. The road sign is the best way to signal the buffer to current and past drivers, though older and current non government sources describe it. If a road sign states 100km/h, do not aim for 120km/h to reach that speed.

Limit driving to 100 km/hr or less as indicated. Why? Speed limits are subject to change, depending on the road. Electronic signs may also display a minimum limit. There may be varying controls for road conditions, work areas, school zones and other areas. A speed buffer does not mean you will never get a ticket. It can also be a problem if visitors drive outside of Dubai. The rules around speed enforcement, for instance, differ in Abu Dhabi and do not include the 20 km/h buffer method.

When you are [**renting a car for a Dubai trip**](/uae/dubai), there is one really simple rule to follow:

Follow the number shown on the road sign. Do not plan your driving around a camera buffer. That way it is simpler and requires less stress. 

### How do Dubai speed cameras catch rental cars?

The Dubai city has installed speed cameras to detect vehicles breaking the speed rule. There are cameras which are secured to the road. Other camera systems are available for installation in various locations. In the past Dubai has also been employing less visible and mobile camera systems. Don’t think you are safe just because you didnt see a camera anywhere. The vehicle can be recorded by a camera. The car’s plate can then be linked to their traffic record. If you commit a traffic violation in a rental car, the infraction can be assigned to the rental car. Dubai Police traffic services include the ability to search for fines by vehicle plate number, driving license number, or traffic code. Therefore, the best way to stay safe is to avoid learning where all of the cameras are. It is to drive on the road at the maximum speed permitted. 

### What happens if you get a speeding fine in a rental car?

The process is easy to understand.

1. **The violation is recorded**

The vehicle is captured by a speed camera if a speeding offense is detected.

2. **The vehicle is linked to the rental company**

The car is registered to the rental company. The company can therefore become involved in the fine process.

3. **The renter may be identified**

The rental company holds rental agreement and details of the driver. It could leverage this information to find out who was in the car at the time of the infraction.

4. **The fine may be passed to the renter**

Traffic fine could be charged to the renter by the rental company. There will be additional company rates. Some rental companies could charge an administration or processing fee. That will depend on the terms of the rental contract.

The exact process can differ between companies. So, even if you rent a car, you are not protected from traffic fines. Speeding is also just one form of traffic offence. Traffic violations include speeding, mobile phone use during driving, red-light running, parking in the wrong spot, and others, according to the Dubai official website.
