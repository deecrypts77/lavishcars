The brand has been an icon of quality and thrill all the way down to Dubai and Maranello. The ten fastest Ferraris are listed below, cars that have changed the way it was done and created a serious impact in the history of automobiles.

![Ferrari LaFerrari](/images/journal/2025-11-untitled-design-10.webp)

1. ## Ferrari LaFerrari – 218 mph (351 km/h)

The LaFerrari is an iconic hybrid car that incorporates a 6.3-liter V12 and an electric motor to produce 950-hp. It accelerates to 0 to 100km/h in only 2.6 seconds. It reaches the top speed of 218 mph and demonstrates the direction of Ferrari in the future hypercar design with the help of advanced aerodynamics and technologies.

2. ## Ferrari SF90 Stradale – 211 mph (340 km/h)

The SF90 Stradale presents the jump of Ferrari into the hybrid power. Its acceleration time is 2.5 seconds thanks to its 986 hp generated by twin-turbo V8 and three electric motors enabling it to accelerate 0 to 100km/h. It is not merely quick, but it is also futuristic, with an electric accuracy, and vintage Ferrari essence.

3. ## Ferrari Enzo – 217 mph (350 km/h)

The Enzo emerged out of Formula 1 and was named after [the founder](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Enzo-Ferrari). Its 6.0-liter V12 generates 651 hp and the carbon-fiber body provides quick handling. This is an eternal supercar that re-established the legacy of Ferrari at the beginning of the 2000s.

4. ## Ferrari 812 Superfast – 211 mph (340 km/h)

As its name suggests, the 812 Superfast offers insane power. One of the most powerful front-engine Ferraris has a 6.5 l naturally aspirated V12 which produces 789 hp. It accelerates 0 to 100km/h in 2.8 seconds combining tradition Ferrari with new technology.

5. ## Ferrari F8 Tributo – 211 mph (340 km/h)

F8 Tributo celebrates the illustrious Ferrari mid-engine V8 tradition. It has a top speed of 211mph and 710 hp, a real performance compliment. It has good aerodynamics and a better weight distribution that makes it exciting when used on the track as well as on the open roads in Dubai.

6. ## Ferrari 488 Pista – 211 mph (340 km/h)

The Ferrari 488 Pista is the purest track car of the company. Pista is Italian for track and the car does not lie. Its twin-turbo V8 is 3.9 liters and produces 710 hp and takes 2.8 seconds to reach 100km/h. It is a pure adrenaline machine due to its light weight and nimble handling.

7. ## Ferrari Daytona SP3 – 211 mph (340 km/h)

Daytona SP3 is also a limited-edition car that commemorates the racing history of Ferrari in the 1960s. It is powered with 6.5-L V12 generating 828 hp and reaching the top speed of 211 mph. It is also fast: every single detail on the car is a testament to the racing legacy.

8. ## Ferrari F12tdf – 211 mph (340 km/h)

The F12tdf is named after the Tour de France and combines brute power and exclusivity. A 6.3-L V12 provides 769 hp and the time between 0 to 100km/h is 2.9 seconds. The automobile is excessive and violent and a dream of every driver.

9. ## Ferrari 599 GTO – 208 mph (335 km/h)

The 599 GTO made a contemporary grand-tourer a track monster. Its V12 6.0-liter generation makes 661 hp, and its aerodynamics replicate the 599XX race car. The consequence is a maximum speed rate of 208mph and a memorable acceleration.

10. ## Ferrari GTC4Lusso – 208 mph (335 km/h)

The GTC4Lusso is a new representation of luxury performance. This four-seat retains its 680 hp with a 6.3L V12, and accelerates to 208mph. It is ideal to those who require fast speed and daily use, best suited to a Dubai vacation.
