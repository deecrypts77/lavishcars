## Dubai’s Roads Are Modern, But The Rules Are Strict 

Some of the most profound shocks to visitors of the UAE traffic law is its enforcement. Compared to other places, Dubai has a well integrated road system. Highways, major roads and intersections are monitored via camera throughout the day. The tickets are automatically issued, and fines may be steep.

One of the reasons for Dubai’s efficient traffic flow, even with the city of cool climate having millions of residents and visitors sharing the road every day, is the rigid approach used. People may think that minor traffic offences or changes of lane out of their way aren’t monitored, but they have a wrong thought. Knowing and respecting the rules from the start make the whole thing a lot more fun.

## Speed Limits Matter More Than Many Visitors Expect 

Among the most frequent driver errors made by foreign drivers is speeding. The roads in Dubai can seem wide and unclogged, leading some drivers to think that they can drive quicker than they should be able to. However, all speed limits should be adhered to. A system of high tech camera technology is in place to detect violations on the city’s roads. Visitors unfamiliar with local regulations can review official driving guidance and traffic laws through the[**&#x20;UAE Government Road Safety Portal**](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/road-safety), which provides important information on safe driving practices and legal requirements. Costs can mount up and repeated offences can result in more serious consequences. Many visitors get surprised on how often dubai speed limits monitoring goes on. It is possible to pass more than one camera checkpoint while driving across the city. A simple strategy is always to pay heed to signs upkeep and maintain a fixed pace through and also your travel.

## Lane Discipline Is Taken Seriously 

There are a lot of countries out there with relatively loose lanes. Dubai is different. Drivers should try to stay in their lane and not cross unnecessary lanes while driving. A sudden lane change, making multiple lanes, or improper signals can be a hazardous road maneuver. The left most road is normally for higher speeds. Slow moving in this lane may cause annoyance and stress to other drivers and occupants. Smooth, predictable driving is great to have. Being an aware, attentive driver that plans ahead for safe lane changes will help make the road a little easier to drive on. 

## Mobile Phone Use Can Be Costly 

Smartphones are the essential tools for modern travellers for navigation, communication, geocoding and travelling arrangements. Using a hand held, mobile device while operating an motor vehicle in any way is prohibited, however, and if caught can bring the big economic punishment. Lots of visitors are tempted to glance at directions or reply to messages from a stopped vehicle. Local regulation still considers this to be a violation. Always plan your trip ahead, use hands free navigation if available, and concentrate on the road. Spending a couple of seconds in a safe preparation routs can positively save a few dollars along the lines of the trip.

## Seat Belts Are Mandatory For Everyone 

This might sound simplistic but it is one of the most forgotten with friends, family members or colleagues visiting the site. All vehicle occupants must be properly secured at all times to the vehicle. It’s not just the duty of individual passengers. Everyone in the vehicle should be in compliance with safety standards and the driver is responsible for them. Children’s transportation must also comply with local Safety laws and regulations. Families travelling with younger children should understand how their child is to be transported prior to any trip. That’s why there are safety laws, and they can help keep all road users safe.

## Red Lights Are Never Worth The Risk 

The violation of traffic rules, such as running a red light is regarded as one of the most severe traffic offences occurring in Dubai. Some visitors come from areas where drivers at times ignore the red light or try to beat the lights. Such a response may result in serious levels of sanctions. Violations are frequently automatically detected and many intersections throughout the city are closely monitored. The penalties can go beyond just financial fines and can be a real set back on your travels. Wait for as long as it takes you when you arrive at an intersection. A few extra seconds sake are much better than suffering the aftermath of a potential error.

## Parking Rules Are Enforced Consistently 

Parking is usually not difficult in Dubai; however, some knowledge of regulations is essential. There are paid parking systems in many places and enforcement is regular. Occasionally, visitors think that short stops, or brief errands, won’t be noticed. Sadly, parking tickets may be issued because of a parking violation. Before parking, ask if there is a charge for parking and confirm any restrictions on parking. A little bit of trailmarking now will save lots of frustration later.

## Driving During Busy Hours Requires Extra Patience 

A good road system is impressive in Dubai but as said in any great city of the world, traffic related issues can happen during rush hours. Urban areas typically witness busier roads during morning and evening rush hours, and in areas with business activity or highways. During these times, some international visitors tend to misjudge the travel times. Consider taking longer for meetings, airport transfers and even critical appointments so as to experience a reduction in stress and a better overall journey. One of the most important skills that one can learn as a driver is patience.

## Weather Can Affect Driving More Than Expected 

People typically think of lots of sunshine when they think of Dubai. The weather is usually fine but can alter from time to time. Dust storms and wind, fine visibility and lack of good rains can create even unusual driving conditions, especially for visitors who have never experienced desert weather. Promoting slowdown and adding extra space cushion is crucial during periods of reduced visibility. Traveler Safety can be enhanced by weather related caution for all on the road.
