## Authentic Muscle Car Experience

The other significant reason why Dodge is popular is its authenticity. When other brands have transitioned to smaller engines and more refined performance, Dodge is still living up to the classic formula of a muscle car. The roar of the engine, the experience of a rear wheel drive, and the immediate acceleration all come into play to make the experience feel pure and unfiltered. And this is what serious car lovers will treasure. Dodge does not aim to recreate the muscle car experience, it makes it.

## Practical Performance for Everyday Use

Making Dodge all the more attractive is the fact that it is a blend of performance and practicality. Most high performance cars compromise on comfort, but the Dodge models are very spacious in their interior and easy to use. An example of this is the Dodge Charger which offers four doors with plenty of seating space and yet offers stunning speed. On the same note, the Dodge Durango SRT provides SUV-comfort, with muscle car performance. This is a balance that makes Dodge a good choice among renters that would desire excitement without sacrificing usability.
