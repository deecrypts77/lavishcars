## Heritage district: The legendary Sheikh Zayed Road

This is the bloodstream of the city–and not without reason. Sheikh Zayed Road is a road that runs parallel to the metro line and leads you to the core of Dubai. Having eight lanes that run in both directions and with as smooth asphalt as can possibly be, it is one of the favorite highways amongst supercar enthusiasts.

Drive past:

- Burj Khalifa
- Dubai World Trade Centre
- Museum of the Future

Ideal when: Driving at night, when there is a view of the skyline; it suits convertibles, such as the Ferrari Portofino.

Tips of the day: Drive during sunset time so that you can capture a particular light scene of the city that will give a cinematic effect to your video content.

## The picturesque route through Al Qudra Road

Feeling like open roads and without traffic? Al Qudra road has long and straight roads where you can feel the strength of your supercar. This road is surrounded by natural sand dunes and desert wildlife, which gives you that feeling of the freedom of the open road, 30 minutes downtown.

Stop at:

- Al Quadra Lake-This one is perfect to take photos of the sunset or sunrise.
- Love Lakes- Ideal detour for romance.

And the best for: Speed-palker cars, such as the [McLaren 720S](/uae/dubai#fleet), [Lamborghini Aventador](/uae/dubai#fleet), or the [Porsche 911 Turbo](/uae/dubai#fleet).

Elegant Tip: Visit early morning or late afternoon when there is perfect light and more comfortable cooler.

## The Ultimate Thrill: Jebel Jais Mountain Road

Although based in Ras Al Khaimah, and it takes a 90-minute drive to get to it from Dubai, [Jebel Jais](https://visitjebeljais.com/) is one of the most thrilling roads in the UAE. It has more than 30 hairpin turns and elevation shifts, and it is ideal for any car enthusiast looking to indulge in performance handling to the fullest.

The fact that it is epic is because 

- It is the tallest in the UAE.
- Mountainous snakes
- Small traffic roads that are wide open

Recommended to: Ferrari F8 Tributo, Aston Martin Vantage, or Audi R8 with high performance.

Tips: You can easily rent your car out to spend a full weekend there, visiting Jebel Jais in the daytime and spending a night in a luxurious resort nearby.

## Palm Jumeirah Crescent Road 

The Crescent Road in Palm Jumeirah is a small but enchanted path that combines a view of the sea and luxury. This can be used most appropriately in cruising, with the roof down, where one can view the best resorts, secluded beaches, and spectacular views of the Dubai Marina skyline.

Drive past:

- **Atlantis The Palm**
- **The Royal Atlantis**
- **W Dubai – The Palm**

Most suitable to: Convertible or exotic attention-getters such as the Lamborghini Huracan Spyder or Rolls-Royce Dawn.

Lavish Tip: Best to be used on the early evening drives before dinner reservation at Nobu or Ossiano.

## The Hatta Road Road From City to Mountains

Hatta Road is a place to go in itself if you have a day or a weekend. Even though this path is more cautious because of the curvier roads closer to the destination, the landscape changes drastically, and the city skyscrapers change to be replaced by calm mountains.

Hatta is wonderful:

- Kayaking
- Mountains walks
- Cultural tours of Hatta Heritage Village

Most appropriate car: A more solid supercar, such as the Lamborghini Urus or G-Wagon, adds speed to the ruggedness of an SUV.

Luxurious Tip: Lavish Cars will recommend luxury SUVs in case you are interested in driving the Hatta trails.

## BR to Dubai Marina- Cruise in Style

Bright, posh, and packed (in a very good sense of this word), this path across Dubai Marina and Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) is intended to be enjoyed by people who like to be noticed.

What you are going to encounter:

- Beautiful waterfront roadsides

- A valet parking corner to corner

Fits: Ferrari 488 Spider, Lamborghini Aventador Roadster, or even a Bugatti Chiron (unless you want to show off).

Extravagant Tip: It is all about flexibility in this route, get dressed, rev smartly, and keep your windows down.

## What You Need to Know Before You Hit the Road

The Laws (or Regulations) of Driving. Safety is the priority, even on the supercar. These are some reminders:

- Wear your seatbelt all the time.
- UAE has speed limits, and they are imposed through radars- keep within the limits.
- No drifting, racing, and crazy lane shoving; penalties are severe.
- Always have the required Documents for Tourists

To have a good experience of a luxury car in Dubai, possess the following:

- International driver’s license (IDP)
- A copy of the passport and the stamping of the visa
- Deposit a credit card
