## Factors Influencing Rental Price

The Continental GT and Flying Spur models and choices influence the car’s pricing, which in turn affects the daily rental cost due to their potent V8 engines, which are not less expensive than the powerful V12 and W12 machines.

Another important factor is the day of the week. On Tuesdays, rather than on Saturdays, when car rental rates are typically higher, I’ve noticed that you may get a better deal.

Even if the older versions demand about 1800 dirhams a day, the daily fee starts as low as 2000 dirhams. If you’re renting a recent model, you could expect to pay about 3000 dirhams a day, though this will vary depending on the company and the season. Possibly they might offer seasonal discounts.

The location of the rental is also important. Due to the extremely crowded as well as unsafe airport parking and driving, renting a car from Dubai International Airport may result in higher rates for insurance. However, if you pick up the vehicle at a city office, your insurance costs can go down.

Additionally, when you hire a Bentley, they will verify your age and driving history, which can have a big impact on not just whether you can rent a Bentley but also how much it will cost.
