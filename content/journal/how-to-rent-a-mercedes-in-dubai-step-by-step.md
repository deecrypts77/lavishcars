In the face of practicality against comfort, the [Mercedes GLS-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet) or [Mercedes V-Class](/uae/dubai#fleet) cannot be defeated. Want to turn heads? Test drive a Mercedes AMG GT, C63 AMG, or G-Class (G63). These models are performance, sport design, and powered roar. It will make you feel like a star when you are riding along the Sheikh Zayed Road or riding along the Marina.

In the case of Mercedes rentals in Dubai, the price of rental may vary between AED 500/day (sedans such as the A-Class) and AED 3,000 and higher/day (G63s and AMG performance cars).

- Insurance cover (comprehensive or basic)
- Mileage limit (often 250–300 km/day)

## Step 3: Check out Rental Requirements 

Dubai does not have complex car rental regulations; still, it is time-saving to be ready. 

If You’re a Tourist, You’ll need:

- A valid passport
- A visit visa
- A domestic driving license.
- International driving permit (IDP).

If You’re a UAE Resident, You’ll need:

- Emirates ID
- [UAE driving license](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/transportation/get-a-driving-licence)
- Verified credit card (to post security deposit)
