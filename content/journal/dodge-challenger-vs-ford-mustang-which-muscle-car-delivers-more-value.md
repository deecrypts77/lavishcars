All great cars have a story to tell. Some are only designed for basic travel and others aslo ensure that you have fun every drive. The Dodge Challenger and the Ford Mustang are in the second group. For decades, these two American muscle cars have had their heads turning. They are known for their bold designs and powerful performance with their unforgettable driving experience. Even with many new sports cars on the market, they continue to be favourites among car lovers around the world. At first, they may seem almost the same. Both have two doors. Both have good engines. Both add a lot of fun to the road. But if you pay closer attention you will see that every car has its own personality.

## Dodge Challenger

The [**Dodge Challenger**](/uae/dubai#fleet) is one of the most popular American muscle cars in history. It was first introduced in 1970 and immediately made its name for its daring looks and impressive performance. The Challenger has remained the same over the years, whereas other cars have undergone major transformation. The first thing people notice is its classic look. It has a broad body with a long bonnet and a strong front grille which gives it an old style muscle car look. The Challenger is also a very heavy car in its class. It has a comfortable interior than many muscle cars, so it is safe to sit for a long time or to fit passengers. The large boot also provides more storage for luggage and carts, or traveling items.

## Ford Mustang

A Ford Mustang is one of the most popular sports cars in the world. It has been around since 1964, and is known for its style, speed and amazing performance. It is still one of the greatest cars ever produced by Ford and continues to grow in popularity with every generation. The design of the Mustang is more modern than Challenger. It has a sleek body, sporty shape and sharp headlights, making it look fast and athletic. It looks ready to move even when standing still. The Mustang is driver oriented, inside. The seats provide good support, and the controls are placed within easy reach. This provides for a sports driving driver position that many enjoy. The Mustang is in particular popular for its driving experience. This makes driving more exciting and interesting, even on the local town road or the open highway.

### Fuel Economy Running Costs

A muscle car is more than just a performance car. There’s a high cost of fuel, maintenance, and routine expenses, particularly if you intend to make frequent trips in the car. Challenger and Mustang are fun cars but when it comes to maintenance, there’s a big difference.

### Dodge Challenger

The Dodge Challenger is the more costly vehicle to operate, particularly with a bigger engine. The Mustang typically consumes less gas than the larger and heavier new models. Servicing is very simple, but for a high performance car it can be expensive. The Challenger is a worthwhile option if comfort and performance are more important than fuel efficiency. 

### Ford Mustang

Ford Mustang generally consumes less gas, especially the smaller sized engine cars. It is also available in a lighter version which can be a good choice for fuel efficiency while maintaining performance. The regular maintenance is similar to the Challenger but with its lower fuel consumption, the Mustang can be more budget friendly for regular drivers.
