Consider touring along Sheikh Zayed Road in an elegantly crafted electric car, with the [Burj Khalifa](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Burj-Khalifa) as a backdrop, and not the engine roar of a car, but instead, the breath of a new future. European royalty like Bentley, BMW, Mercedes and Rolls-Royce is what [luxury car rental in Dubai](/) signified until recently. However, a new upstart has arrived to change things: BYD (Build Your Dreams).

Exactly, BYD, the Chinese electric vehicle powerhouse, is bringing the high-end experiences at not so much high end prices. In this blog, we contrast [BYD EV rental services](/uae/dubai#fleet) against the old European car companies and address a single all important question of Dubai visitors: Can the great be affordable and eco-friendly?

## What Is BYD and Why Is It Gaining Popularity in Dubai?

BYD (Build Your Dreams) is the largest EV manufacturer in the world—yes,4 times as large as Tesla in global sales. BYD is cheap and innovative and has incredible battery technology, which has already started a disruption within the Dubai rental business environment.

**BYD New Models to Hire:**

- BYD Tang EV JAT-7-seater EV version with sport performance
- BYD Han EV: SUV-level sedan, stylish and with high speed
- BYD Song Plus DM-i-PK -Long-range travel plug-in hybrid crossover
- BYD Seal- Slim mid-size electric sedan that offers specification like Tesla Model 3

BYD has become the secret weapon of the smart renter with the active support of the UAE government on green mobility issues and the growing amount of the EV infrastructure available in Dubai.

## Eco-Conscious Driving in Dubai

Sustainability is becoming one of the trends among residents and tourists. Since the Dubai Clean Energy Strategy 2050 is well underway, the renting of an EV is not only a fad but also wise.

- Zero emissions
- Free/subsidized parking to EVs
- Green zones: access to them
- Competitive charging fees, which are low (charging only AED 5 in some stations of DEWA)

BYD is the reigning champion in terms of eco-performance, with a 400-550 km range on one charge that is quite appropriate to visit:

- Hatta Mountains
- Al Qudra desert
- Abu Dhabi Corniche

## Maintenance Peace of Mind

The thing is, luxury rentals are not all about the drive. They are concerned with trust. This is how BYD will make your renting easier:

- Less mechanical hazards (the EVs have fewer moving parts)
- No oil-changing
- Regeneration brake system = lengthened lifespan
- Any problem is signaled using battery management system alerts

However, premium European cars can have:

- The need to be serviced often
- Greater costs of repairing any damage
- Technicians were required to be specialized.
