Dubai and Saudi Arabia are associated not only with the frontiers, but with cultural aspects, business, and travelling. Driving a rental limo car into Saudi Arabia is more than just a trip to some people traveling and residing in the UAE, as it is, a brand of excellence and comfort. Just imagine rolling through the golden sands on a Bentley Flying Spur or charging through the long highways on a Lamborghini Urus, then every pit stop is a luxury experience.

However, here is the twist, when you rent a car in Dubai, you cannot drive it to Saudi Arabia with press of the button! There are regulations, papers and expenses that you need to cross the T and check before you get started.

In this article, you will find an ultimate checklist all you need to know to drive your rental car throughout Dubai to Saudi Arabia in the year 2025, legally, safely, and comfortably.

## Why Drive from Dubai to Saudi Arabia in a Rental Car?

The question arises why many people adopt this path.

- Comfortable to Business Travelers

A lot of executives fly into Dubai, rent a high-end car such as a [Mercedes S Class](/uae/dubai#fleet) or Range Rover Vogue and drive directly to Riyadh or Dammam to meet. No waiting at the airport, no baggage.

- Beautiful Road Trips

The sandy deserts of UAE merge into the expanses of Saudi Arabia. This is an experience that you would not get when you are flying, driving allows you to experience this transformation.

- Flexibility to Pilgrims and Families

The driving also enables family groups going to Makkah and Madinah to ride together and to make rest stops during travel.

- Luxury Lifestyle

Nothing can be more prestigious than to arrive on a destination in a Rolls-Royce Cullinan or a Bentley Continental GT.

## The Checklist

The following are all the things you need to successfully and legally cross the UAE-Saudi border with your rental car in Dubai.

### 1. Confirm Cross-Border Permission with Your Rental Company

Not every car rental company in Dubai can travel across the border. Expensive cars and supercars, such as Ferrari and McLaren are usually restricted under its insurance and mileage restrictions.

In [Lavish Cars](/), some luxury SUVs and sedans are not allowed to travel on the Saudi roads, but once approved in writing prior to the trip, they are. Always ask:

- Can Saudi Arabia be traveled through this rental?”
- Do you have any additional charges involved in cross border insurance?”
- What is the mileage allowance on overseas journeys?”

Pro Tip: SUVs such as the Range Rover Autobiography or Mercedes G63 are trendy since they are more comfortable on the highway at long distances and easier to get approved in Saudi travel.

###  2. Required Documents for UAE-Saudi Travel

Travelling across borders is not only about the passport but a set of documents. Here is the list of things you must have:

- Valid Passport with not less than 6 months validity.
- The type of Saudi Visa you require: a tourist, business or Hajj/Umrah, or a transit visa, depending on the purpose of the Saudi Visa you are seeking.
- International or UAE Driving License (depending on the resident/ tourist status).
- Vehicle Registration (Mulkiya) -provisions must be made by the company renting the vehicle.
- Cross-Border Authorization Letter of your rental agency.
- Insurance Papers must be valid in Saudi Arabia.

These are necessary to travel without ending up at the border.

###  3. Cross-Border Insurance Coverage

Normal UAE car insurance does not include automatic coverage in Saudi Arabia. You will be required to have GCC wide insurance.

- The rental agencies normally provide:
- Daily and weekly Saudi insurance supplements.
- GCC insurance packages on longer stays.

Example: In case you hire a Bentley Bentayga in Riyadh to go on a 5-day business trip, you will have to pay additionally AED 300-500 on Saudi insurance.

### 4. Roadside Assistance and Emergency Support

The highway line DUBAI to SAUDI is long and wide. Good as it sounds low maintenance is still possible in premium rentals, even though the breakdowns are uncommon. Make sure you always ask your rental provider

- Does your company provide 24 hours roadside assistance in Saudi Arabia?
- In case of breakdown across the border do you offer a replacement of car?

The SUVs and the hybrids may serve you better in long-haul trips than the sports cars that have low clearance.

### 5. Vehicle Choice: Which Car Is Best for the Trip?

Your car is not all about the appearance but rather the practicability when on the road.

- SUVs( Range Rover, Cadillac Escalade, Mercedes G-Wagon)
- Specifically designed to be comfortable, spacious and long-distance driving.
- Luxury Sedans ([Bentley Flying Spur](/uae/dubai#fleet), Mercedes S-Class, BMW 7-Series)
- Ideal to use with families or those executive people who want luxury and speed.
- Luxury Cars (Bentley, Maserati, Aston Martin)
- Not preferable but possible – low clearance, high fuel consumption, and possible limited insurance opportunities.

Pro Tip: Bentley Continental GT Convertible is a luxury car, but it is road-trip ready and cosy.

### 6. Border Crossing Points

The most visited crossings are

- Al Batha Border Crossing -Main route between the UAE and Saudi (Abu Dhabi–Riyadh).
- Al Ghuwaifat Border Post -A popular route of travel especially by business and family travelers.

Be ready to have queue especially during Hajj and holidays.

### 7. Fuel and Costs

Fuel is less expensive in Saudi Arabia than in the U.A.E. This also makes out and back trips less expensive.

- UAE Petrol Price (2025 est.): AED 3.00 per liter.
- Saudi Petrol Price (2025 pt.) : SAR 2.30 per liter aprox. (\~AED 2.25)

You will save money on a full tank for a Range Rover Vogue to fill up once you are in Portugal.

### 8. Travel Etiquette & Road Rules in Saudi Arabia

- Speed Limits:The speed on highways varies between 120 and 140 km/h.
- Seatbelts are compulsory among passengers.
- No alcohol- like Dubai, the penalties are very strong.
- Women have the right to drive- a change in recent years, but ID and paperwork should be at hand all the time.

Pro Tip: Never forget to refer to Google Maps even when in Saudi territory, to get checkpoints and traffic availability.

### 9. Costs You Should Expect

In addition to rental and fuel, allow a budget to:

- [Border Exit Fees](https://blog.wego.com/saudi-exit-re-entry-visa/) (where such fees apply).
- Extra Kilometers (most of the rentals consist of a daily kilometers allowance).
- Insurance Add-ons.
- Exit Dubai LiK (Toll) Fees.

As an example, a Bentley Bentayga hire at AED 2,800/day could add Saudi insurance and mileage extras to add up to about AED 3,300/day.
