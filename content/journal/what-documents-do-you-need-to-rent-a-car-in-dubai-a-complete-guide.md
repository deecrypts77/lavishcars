The residents and tourists of the UAE have different car rental requirements in Dubai. The residents of the UAE should possess a UAE driving license, an Emirati ID, a residence permit, and a copy of their passport. Renters of tourist cars are required to provide their home country and a foreign driving license, a visitor license, and a copy of their passport. In Dubai, [renting a car](/) can also occur with a driver or chauffeur to make the process convenient for renters. This guide has everything that you require to know about the required documents, tourist and resident additional requirements, and renting suggestions.

## Required Documents for Renting a Car in Dubai

When hiring a car in Dubai, UAE citizens are required to have a driver’s license that is valid for at least 12 months. It provides insurance, legal protection, and checks the identity and residency of the renter. Under the rental car regulation in the UAE, an Emirates ID will also be required for residency verification, insurance claims, and financial transactions. An extended period of residency involves a resident visa, which contains personal information and a copy of the passport. The documents provide legal and administrative compliance. 

### 1. Passport or Emirates ID

The most elementary but the most significant thing that tourists must have to drive a car in Dubai is a valid passport. Your passport will be your most important identification, and it should have a minimum of six months before expiry of your passport, starting with your planned departure date in Dubai. Tourists are also required to have either a valid on-entry visa or a tourist visa, depending on their nationality. During travel, the majority of tourists are able to acquire a visa after their landing activities in [Dubai International Airport](https://dubaiairports.ae/corporate/our-story/dxb-dubai-international), or acquire it online. Before making your plans to go to Dubai, it is necessary to make sure of your visa requirements depending on your nationality.

### 2. International Driving Permit (IDP) 

The tourists who wish to drive in Dubai need to have an International Driving Permit (IDP). An IDP is an internationally recognized interpretation of your country’s driver’s license to drive legally in different countries. Even when you have a valid driver’s license in your home country, the authorities of Dubai demand that you have an IDP together with your original driver’s license. An IDP can be easily acquired in most countries, and it is recommended that you apply when you are back home to prevent last-minute hitches.

### 3. Valid Driver’s License

Besides the IDP, the tourist should have their original driver’s license issued by their country of residence. The original driver’s license must be clear and not expired. It should be noted that other car rental organizations might require further identification to confirm the authenticity of the driver, e.g., a national identity card or a credit card with the name of the client renting the vehicle.

### 4. Credit Card for Security Deposit

The majority of car rental companies in Dubai ask tourists to submit a valid credit card under the name of the main driver. A security deposit is taken from the credit card, and the rental company holds this security deposit until the customer returns the rented car. It is a mandatory pre-authorization of the credit card in the form of a security deposit, which can be withdrawn after returning the car without any violations or damages to the vehicle. The security deposit usually differs among various car rental companies and according to the different car rental vehicles.

### 5. Proof of Insurance

Dubai car rental companies mostly offer basic insurance coverage on the rented cars. It is, however, important that the tourists be informed on the nature and extent of insurance cover that they have under their rental agreement. Their typical insurance cover usually covers third-party liability insurance that compensates the victim in the case of an accident, including damage to property and other vehicles. The tourists would also have a choice of purchasing other insurance covers, including collision damage waiver (CDW) and theft protection, to reduce their financial burden due to an accident or theft.

## Age Requirements for Renting a Car in Dubai

Tourists who wish to rent a car in Dubai should also be above the minimum car rental age, which is normally 21 years old. But there are chances that a rental agency might demand that the driver be 25 years old before renting. It is important to make sure that you remind the age limit of the car rental company that you are planning to use before making a reservation. Also, anyone under the age of 25 will likely have to pay a young driver surcharge, and this can add up to the total charge.

## Do You Need an International Driving Permit (IDP) in Dubai?

Your driver’s license must be in either English or Arabic; otherwise, you are required to get an International Driving Permit (IDP) before you arrive in Dubai. An IDP translates your license to several languages and is accepted globally. But, when you already have a license in English (licenses of the US, UK, Australia, or EU countries), there is no necessity to have it in IDP.

## Additional Requirements for Tourists

### Traffic Violations and Driving History 

The drivers who rent a car in Dubai must have a clear driving history without recent traffic violations. Certain hiring agencies could do a verification on the driving record of the driver before securing the rental vehicle. The rental application may be denied in case of certain traffic offenses, which may include speeding or reckless driving. That is why, before you decide to hire a car in Dubai, make sure that your driving record does not contain any significant offenses.

### Pre-Booking and Reservation 

Tourists are advised to make a booking for their car hire in Dubai to get the most competitive rates and ensure car availability. Online booking helps tourists to compare rates, find the most suitable car, and look at the terms and conditions of the rental. Furthermore, pre-booking makes it possible not to spend time in the queue at the rental counter when arriving at the airport and deal with car rental worry-free.

### Rental Agreement and Terms 

Before renting cars, tourists should read the rental agreement and terms offered by the car rental companies carefully. The conditions of the rental agreement are the period of rent, the rent fee, the insurance cover, and other fees or charges. The terms and conditions must be known to avoid any surprises or misunderstandings involving the rental process.

### Awareness of Road Rules and Regulations

The visitors intending to drive in Dubai city are recommended to understand the rules and regulations on the roads of the city. The traffic in Dubai follows the right-hand drive, and the use of seat belts is obligatory for both passengers and drivers. Speeding, aggressive driving also have severe laws that impose high fines. The tourists should also be educated on the available parking space and not to park where they are not supposed to, get a fine.
