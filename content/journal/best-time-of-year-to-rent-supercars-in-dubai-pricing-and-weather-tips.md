Dubai is a place where luxury grows. It is a place where there are unbelievable experiences like skyscrapers, five star hotels and islands that are privately owned. However, one excitement supersedes them and it is the driving of a supercar through the iconic streets of Dubai. Driving a Lamborghini Huracan on the Sheikh-Zayed Road, Rolls-Royce down Jumeirah Beach Road, Bentley Continental GT on the outskirts of the desert; no matter which supercar is rented in Dubai, driving it is not only a means of transport, but a rite.

And the question is this, when is the best time to hire super car in Dubai?

It is not only availability. It takes into consideration weather, prices, seasonal demand and even the kind of trip you intend to have. Here in this guide, I will simplify it all using real life tips, personal experiences and insider secrets to help you save money and make your experience even more awesome.

## Why Timing Matters in Supercar Rentals

Most tourists suppose that all through the year, [renting a supercar in Dubai](/) is done at constant rates. No that is not the case. The same way with hotels and air tickets car rental rates vary depending on winter or summer seasons, weather and events.

These are the main factors that influence when you ought to rent:

1. Climate in Dubai: No one will go to drive a [Ferrari Spider](/uae/dubai#fleet) in 45 heats.
2. Tourist Season The more visitors, the more demand and the more price.
3. Events and Holidays events and holidays such as the Expo or Formula 1 in Abu Dhabi, or New Year Eve may cause the rates to soar.
4. Midweek/weekend – As with hotels, renting midweek can save you a nice bit of money.

## Supercar Rentals in Dubai by Season

![Supercar Rentals in Dubai](/images/journal/2025-09-untitled-design-18-1.webp)

Let us take it a month at a time so you are aware of what to expect.

### 1. Winters ( (November- February): The High Season

- Weather: 18c to 28c which is a perfect weather to travel by convertible/outdoors.
- Pricing: At highest of the year because of high demand.
- Ferrari Portofino, Lamborghini Huracan Spyder, Rolls-Royce Dawn best cars of the season.
- It is the prime time. Consider driving down to [palm Jumeirah](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g295424-d660445-Reviews-Palm_Jumeirah-Dubai_Emirate_of_Dubai.html) on a sunny day with the top being lowered, with no feeling of heat and only wind on your skin.

Pro Tip: Book 3-4 weeks ahead of time, at least, in the case of December or January visits. Rates may get as much as 20 to 40 percent higher during Christmas, New Year and the Dubai Shopping Festival.

### 2. Spring (March May): the Sweet Spot

- Weather: Nice initially (22 to 30 degree centigrade) but May can hit the 38 degree centigrade mark.
- Pricing: More equitable, running a bit below the winter.
- Season Cars of the Year: Bentley Continental GT (excellent cooling when it is warm), Aston Martin DB11, McLaren 720S.
- Experience: The best balance ever-warm, yet manageable weather, along with thinner crowds.

I remember a client who took a Bentley continuity GT on hire in March to have a road trip to the Hatta Dam. It was cool in the mountain roads in the mornings and he said this was the most James Bond moment of his life. The months of spring are also best to take longer trips out of the city.

### 3. Summer (June- September): Affordable, but Hot

- Climate: Extremes of heat (above 40 6C). Least ease to humidity compounds.
- Pricing: The cheapest rates all year round and in many cases 30-50 per cent cheaper in summer as compared to winter.
- Best Cars of the Season- Coupe super cars with good A/C Lamborghini Urus, Bentley Flying Spur, Porsche 911 Turbo.
- Experience: The mid-day is hectic and the evenings magical. Sheik Zayed Road at night looks like a racetrack.

Pro Tip: If you are making a visit during summer, hire a chauffeur driven vehicle. You can feel the luxury aspect without the fear of getting heats exhaustion.

### 4. Autumn(October): The Month of Transition

- Climate: The temperature falls to 30 o C 35 o C, which is bearable during the day road travel.
- Prices: A little above the summer but below the peak winter.
- The best vehicles to buy in this season include Lamborghini Aventador S, Ferrari 488 GTB, and Rolls Royce Cullinan.

October is one of the best-kept secrets. Temperature is tolerable with the city just preparing to go into high season and the rental fleets are fresh.

## How Pricing Works: Seasonal Hacks

- Daily v. Hourly Rentals: Hourly rates (e.g. 2-4hrs) might seem cheaper however when on a full-day road trip to Abu Dhabi or Hatta a daily is more economical.
- Book Midweek: During Tuesdays and Wednesdays, the rates are usually cheaper by 10 15 percent in contrast to the weekends.
- Event Weekends: There will be rushes around Abu Dhabi F1 (November), Eid holidays, and events at the Dubai Expo.
- Reward Programs: A few luxury rental services (such as [Lavish Cars](/)) provide membership discounts on repeat business.

## Best Weather for Different Supercars

- Different vehicles drive (and feel) different in different weather:
- Convertibles (Ferrari Portofino, Rolls-Royce Dawn): Best months of the year Nov-Feb.
- SUV Supercars (Lamborghini Urus, Bentley Bentayga): Ideal solution when it is hot outside and you need to be in the desert.
- Sports Coupes (McLaren 720S, Porsche 911 Turbo): Awesome all-year around but especially at night, in spring and autumn.

## Local Tips to Maximize Your Supercar Rental

- Take Sunset Drives: Golden hour out at sea is spectacular at the Dubai Marina, and Jumeriah Beach Road.
- Make Road Trips: A Bentley Continental GT would be ideal in the mountains of Hatta, a Lamborghini Huracn takes the drive to Abu Dhabi and the Yas Marina straight out of a dream.
- Mileage Allowances: Most rentals only have 250 kms per day so factor in your itinerary especially on a desert or mountain run.
- Insurance Matters: Don t be stingy when it comes to insurance cover. And full cover can save you heartburns especially when used to driving high-performance cars.
- License Requirements: Foreigners should use their local home states license (if it is accepted) or an International Driving Permit (IDP).

## Conclusion

What is the best time of the year to hire supercars in Dubai?

- The best experience: November to February.
- To get moderate prices and climate: March, April or October.
- On a budget: June to September.
