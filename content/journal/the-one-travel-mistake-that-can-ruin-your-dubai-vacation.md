## Why Transportation Matters More in Dubai Than Most Travelers Think 

A number of first time Dubai visitors think that it is like other major cities where the objects are all in one place. The facts are quite the opposite. Dubai is a large city with lots to offer from various destinations. In the morning, visit Downtown Dubai, lunch at Dubai Marina, afternoons at Palm Jumeirah followed by a business meeting in Business Bay. Public transportation is an option, but not always the quickest way to get to your destination. Dubai’s Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) provides extensive public transport services, but planning your transportation in advance can help you save valuable time, especially during peak travel periods. Public transportation is an option, but not always the quickest way to get to your destination. The absence of reliable transportation can make travelling to and from and around a tourist destination very time consuming which can result in lost hours waiting for taxi services, and or the unpredictable surge pricing of ride hailing services, being un familiar with the route of transportation and having to change plans when transportation is delayed.

## The Hidden Costs of Poor Transportation Planning 

The price is the attitude many travelers look at when they make their decision for transportation without taking the bigger picture into account. Initially, using only taxis or ride-sharing services might appear an inexpensive method to get to and from the airport. However, frequent trips throughout the city can quickly add up. In addition to the financial cost, there is a associated cost in convenience.

The time taken to wait for cars during peak hours, attendance issues during events and the additional time spent organising transport can lessen the quality of your holidays.

## How Transportation Problems Create Vacation Stress 

Vacations should be a period of relaxation. Unfortunately, transportation issues can bring a lot of stress. You’ve arranged a dinner date for a very fancy restaurant, but can’t find a ride around rush hour. Imagine sitting home waiting for a planned desert ecolodge tour to arrive and missing it all by the arrival time of the transport. Imagine coming into a significant business meeting stressed and angry. Every time you are on the road, it is a question of how you will get there.When you are in transit, there is pressure all day long. Not only is travel a pleasure, but it is a way of life for some people, travelling from one destination to another keeps them in constant fear of reaching the next.
