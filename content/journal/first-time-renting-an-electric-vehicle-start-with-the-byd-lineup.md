## Charging Your BYD: Where and How

Dubai makes it simple. You can charge BYD EV:

- DEWA Green Chargers (hanging out in most malls, public parking)
- There are hotels like Atlantis, Address Downtown, and [Jumeirah Beach Hotel](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/places-to-visit/jumeirah-beach-hotel).
- Carrefour or Waitrose (some store) supermarket chains
- EV apps such as PlugShare, ChargeMap and DEWA’s EV Green Charger Locator (EV LOKATOR).

### Charging Time Overview:

| Charger Type     | Time to Full Charge (BYD Tang) |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Fast DC (80kW)   | 30–45 minutes                  |
