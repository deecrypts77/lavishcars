## How credit card pre-authorization replaces cash deposits

**A pre-authorization on a credit card is just a temporary hold on the card. It’s like the rental company verifying that you have enough credit or cash on you.**

The amount is temporarily frozen. It is not always taken as a normal payment. Let’s say you’ve booked a car, and the terms of the car rental include that you should have a card hold. The rental company can request a temporary stop of a portion of your available credit from your bank. You are not able to use the amount of money during the hold.

This is different from paying the money to the rental company as a normal charge. If you’re returning the car and everything has been verified, then the rental company can lift the hold as per its terms. As long as there are no additional charges, the money can be available. But the release may not be immediate. A quick release can be managed by the rental company. This may take a little longer for your bank or card issuer to allow the cash back to show up again. This can depend on the bank/card provider. That’s why you need to ask about card hold details before booking. Avoid depending entirely on the words zero deposit. A zero cash deposit rental is not the same as a card pre-authorization.

## What can you still be responsible for? 

**Getting a security deposit back doesn’t guarantee that all rental charges are eliminated, you are responsible for your fines, fuel charges, any damage to car etc.&#xA0;**

This should be stated in your rental contract. Common examples include:

**Vehicle damage**

Depending on your agreement, there may be some damage that you have to cover during the rental. It is recommended to check out the car before travelling and ensure that all existing damage has been noted correctly. You can also take pictures of the vehicle when you’re taking it back in to have a clear record of the condition of that vehicle.

**Traffic fines**

If you get a fine ticket, this can be your responsibility. Understand how fines will be dealt with before embarking on the journey.

**Salik**

Electronic Road Toll (eRT) is called Salik in Dubai. In case you enter the Salik toll point, the corresponding toll amount may be charged as per the terms of the rental.

**Fuel**

Take a look at the fuel condition before you are on your way. The agreement may cover the amount of fuel the car should have on return. A charge may be made if the vehicle is returned with less fuel than is required.

**Late return**

Any change in your work schedule may impact your return time. Let the rental company know as soon as you think you are going to be late. Additional fees may apply, depending on the agreement.

**Cleaning**

Luxury cars are expected to be returned in an acceptable condition. Additional or special cleaning may be required, depending on the vehicle’s condition and may be charged for under the rental conditions.

**Extra mileage**

Some rental agreements may include a travel limit. If your booking has one, check how it is charged before taking a long trip. Specific rules may differ depending on vehicle and booking. Always read the agreement for the car you are renting.
