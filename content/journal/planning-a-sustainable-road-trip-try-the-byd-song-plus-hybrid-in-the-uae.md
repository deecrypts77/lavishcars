## Why Sustainability on the Road Matters in the UAE

As the world increasingly switches to green travel, being sustainable is not a trend; it is a way of life. The UAE is a country of high-octane luxury, but it is also in the forefront of environmental awareness. The efforts toward net-zero construction, electric transportation services, etc. indicate the trend of sustainable living.

And when you have to travel long distances, say, a coastal journey between Abu Dhabi and [Fujairah](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g311321-Activities-Fujairah_Emirate_of_Fujairah.html) or a mountainous one between Hatta, then the vehicle you drive in actually counts. Electric hybrid rentals such as the BYD Song Plus will enable you to maintain a low carbon footprint without compromising on the excitement of vacations.

## Route Inspiration: Sustainable Scenic Drives in the UAE

Have in mind to rent the BYD Song Plus Hybrid and take a ride? These are three carefully selected eco-road trip routes that will make you feel clean in the conscience and Instagram-cluttered.

1. **Dubai Hatta**

- Distance: 130 km one way
- Highlights: Hatta Dam, heritage village, hiking trails
- Tips: In town operate in full EV mode to minimize fuel consumption; move into e-hybrid mode when the terrain becomes steep.

2. **Abu Dhabi Jebel Hafeet, Al Ain**

-  Distance: One-way 170 km
- Highlights: Mountain views, Hot springs, Al Ain Zoo
- Tip: Regen Braking on Downhills to charge the battery.

3. **Fujairah Coastal Escapade Dubai**

- Distance: 180km one-way
- Highlights: Snoopy island, serene beaches, mountain tunnels
- Tip: Pull over on the way and charge your range in fast-charging stations.
