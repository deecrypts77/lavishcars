## Insider Tips for the Best Experience

- Early Booking– Do it ahead of time, especially during events or holidays.
- Inquire About Deposit – Some rentals have an AED 5,000–10,000 hold.
- Mind the Mileage – A lot of cars come with daily caps (e.g., 250 km).
- Avoid Peak Times – Driving during afternoons is quieter compared to overscheduled night hours.
- Take it Easy – Operate all the features on your luxury car and learn how everything functions before you take off.
