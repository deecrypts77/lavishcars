## 1. Luxurious Interior with Smart Tech

Enter the BYD HAN EV and you instantly get a sense of a luxurious and driver-friendly cabin that welcomes. Its interiors have spacious seating with high-quality vegan leather, ambient lighting and a 15.6 inch rotating touchscreen that allows adjustment of landscape and portrait mode making navigation, finding music, or managing car settings easy with a swipe.

The cabin is plush, roomy, and whisper silent due to the sophisticated NVH (Noise, Vibration, Harshness) insulation of BYD. This makes it a very comfortable atmosphere both the driver and its passengers and is perfect during long drives in Dubai or road trips to Abu Dhabi.

- Smart infotainment system
- Cozy interior with technology-orientated interior design

## 2. Outstanding Range and Battery Life

Range anxiety is one of the primary issues people have with EVs, but with the BYD HAN EV, there is nothing to worry about. Having a stunning [range of up to 521 km](https://www.byd.com/eu/blog/BYD-HAN-executive-sedan-spearheading-BYDs-European-range) (WLTP) on a single charge, it is perfect for driving long distances in the UAE without having to pause charging frequently.

It is built using an innovative Blade Battery technology of BYD that not only prolongs the life of the vehicle but also contributes to its safety and efficiency. This EV will not disappoint you whether you devote a whole day of fun in a city or explore desert highways.

- Electric long-range car
- Faster charging EV hire
- Blade Battery to protect and sustain

## 4. High-Performance Dual-Motor AWD

The whites make you think that the BYD HAN EV is going to be a quiet ride, and you would be wrong. It has a dual-motor all-wheel-drive (AWD) system and is capable of producing up to 494 hp and can go 0 to 100 km/h within 3.9 seconds.

Powerful, responsive, and smooth always, whether you drive through the expansive highways of Dubai or through the twists of Jebel Hafeet, the HAN EV gives you a comfortable ride. It is not a luxury vehicle; it is a performance vehicle.

- Fast accelerating electric car
- AWD system to give better grip
- Smooth handling and drive stability
