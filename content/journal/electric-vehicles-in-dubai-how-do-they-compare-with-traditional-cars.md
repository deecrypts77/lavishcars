Did you know that Dubai is aiming for 50% of all vehicles on the road to be electric or hybrid by 2050? The city is investing in more charging stations annuall and promoting a greener mode of transport. Electric vehicles (EVs) are becoming more common in Dubai. They can be seen at shopping malls, hotels, business centers and even tourist sites. Meanwhile, petrol cars are still the main mode of transportation due to their ease of use and instant refuelling options. So, what’s better? What is the smartest option, an electric vehicle or the old petrol car? This will depend on your intentions. Different choices will be made by a tourist, a business traveller and a Dubai resident. It can also depend on other aspects such as driving distance, charging access, fuel costs and convenience.

## What Is an Electric Vehicle?

### How Does an Electric Vehicle Work?

An Electric Vehicle (EV) is a vehicle that uses electricity rather than petrol or diesel. This operates on a rechargeable battery which drives an electric motor. EVs have no fuel engine, so emit no tailpipe pollution and run significantly quieter than a gas powered vehicle.

### How Do You Charge an EV?

EVs can be charged at either residential or public charging stations throughout Dubai. Fast chargers recharge the battery in less than an hour, while normal chargers typically will take longer. With a growing network of charging stations, it becomes increasingly convenient for drivers to charge their vehicles in UAE. [**Dubai Electricity and Water Authority (DEWA)**](https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/) reported that the city was continuously boosting its public network of EV charging stations to meet the demand for EVs. This makes charging much easier compared to a few years ago. 

### Popular Electric Cars in Dubai

Electric models are now available from many leading brands in the UAE. Some of the most popular models are the Tesla, BYD, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, and Kia. There are a variety of sizes and costs in this range of cars, allowing drivers more choices than ever.

## What Is a Traditional Car?

A car that runs on petrol or diesel fuel. It  burns fuel and generates power with an engine. This force is used to move the wheels and gives power to the car. Traditional cars have been the most common type of vehicle for many years. They continue to be a popular option for daily driving and road trips in Dubai, especially for those in search of adventure.

### The working of a Petrol Car

Fuel is stored in a tank of a petrol car. The fuel is used to provide energy in the engine. This energy powers the car & aids in its movements. Once the fuel runs low, you just drop into a petrol station and fill up your tank. The entire procedure typically takes about a few minutes.

### Why Are Petrol Cars Still Popular?

The popularity of petrol cars is due to their simplicity. Drivers need not worry about charging as fuel stations are available all over Dubai. They are also offered in a variety of styles ranging from small city cars to luxury sedans and family SUVs.

### Common Types of Traditional Cars in Dubai

There is a wide range of petrol cars available in Dubai. Sedans are also a popular choice for people who want travel on a regular basis and for business purposes. For families and longer trips, SUVs are the preferred vehicle choice. Visitors interested in an exciting driving experience typically opt for sports cars, whereas luxury cars are preferred for special occasions.

## Running Costs in Dubai

Driving cost is one of the largest factors that people consider when acquiring an electric vehicle. The cost of electricity is typically cheaper than petrol and this cost can make up for the initial price of the EV over time. For those who drive daily, you might find that your energy bills will be reduced, particularly if you charge your car at home or at a public charging station you can pick up that is cheap.

On the other hand, traditional cars rely on fuel prices. The cost of petrol changes every month in the UAE, so your fuel expenses can vary throughout the year. Another thing to consider is your driving style. With a few days car rental Dubai one may not notice much difference in the running cost. ## Driving Experience

The driving experience of both electric and petrol cars is comfortable. The difference is in their road presence.

### Driving an Electric Vehicle

One of the strengths of the EVs is that they are smooth and quiet. Since they don’t have a traditional engine, there is very little noise when driving. They also provide instant acceleration. The car responds promptly as soon as you press its accelerator. This makes driving in the city a no brainer, particularly in traffic jams.

### Driving a Petrol Car

Petrol cars offer the driving experience that people are used to. For many drivers, the sound of the engine, along with the feeling of changing gears is a delight.

They also run well on highway roads and are perfect for long distance trips where rapid fueling is essential.

## Performance in Dubai’s Weather

Many people wonder if electric vehicles can handle Dubai’s hot climate. The answer is yes. The modern EVs are engineered to function in hot weather. They come with cooling systems to help maintain temperatures in them, even in the summer.

### Does Hot Weather Affect Battery Range?

The driving range of an EV could be slightly lowered by extreme heat. When being used for extended periods, the air conditioning system could also consume some battery capacity. This isn’t usually a problem for most daily trips around Dubai. Even though modern electric vehicles have limited ranges, they are still sufficient for city driving. High temperatures also impact petrol cars. You can see that the cooling system is built into the engine to ensure that it won’t overheat, and regular maintenance helps keep the engine in good running order all year. 

## Final Thoughts

Electric vehicles are gaining more ground in Dubai’s future. They have lower running expenses, they drive silently and they don’t have any polluting gases. As the city’s charging network continues to grow, driving an EV is becoming easier than ever. But conventional petrol vehicles are still sensible options. They refuel quickly, are great for traveling long distances and have a wide variety of models. The answer to this question will depend on how you plan to travel, your driving style and personal taste. For Dubai, an electric car or a petrol car can deliver a comfortable and enjoyable driving experience, depending on your preference.
