Dubai is a city that likes to take people aback.

You may have seen the stunning skyline, luxury hotels, world famous shopping malls, and glamorous social media posts. However, Dubai is a different experience to the one tourists dream of. Visitors often come with a preconceived idea of the city they are expecting, only to find a surprising fee or remember the rules they had no idea about the first time or learning about great new spots they missed before.

When it comes to travel, having some secret hacks can make all the difference, whether you’re back for your holiday or travelling for work or other special events. It can save you time, minimize stress, avoid the pitfalls of travel and gives you the opportunity to see the city from the point of view of someone who has been traveling for some time. So, if you have an upcoming travel, these are some of the things that every tourist wants to know before visiting Dubai.

## Dubai Is Much Bigger Than It Looks 

When many first time visitors arrive in Dubai, they think that the city is small and everything is close. After all, photographs can indicate the attractions are next to each other. That is, of course, not the case. Dubai is a sprawling city and popular spots may be quite distant. It can take 20 to 40 minutes to travel between Dubai Marina and Downtown Dubai, depending on the weather and traffic. If you have several attractions to visit, then it’s important to plan for the day.

This is particularly important on holidays, large exhibitions, shopping festivals and during the winter tourist season when the roads are busier than normal. Before you even arrive at your destination, it is important that you know what transportation is available, as it can save you productive vacation hours. The majority of visitors quickly discover that the use of taxis can be costly if they have to travel around a lot.

## The Weather Can Feel More Intense Than You Expect 

A lot of travelers read the forecasted temperature and imagine what it will be like to travel there during that time. The weather of Dubai is not the only aspect of it. Humidity can be important, particularly in summer. A temperature that appears acceptable on a weather application can not seem so comfortable outside.

During the warmer part of the year (June through September) day time temperatures often reach more than 40 °C, and in cooler months they can be pleasantly warm in the heat of the day.

Dubai is designed for comfort and the good news is that it is. There are complete A.C. systems in hotels, malls, restaurants, business centers, transportation facilities and attractions. When people visit Dubai, one of the best times they can expect pleasant weather is during winter seasons, making it one of the reasons Dubai is highly visited by tourists from all over the world.

## Respecting Local Culture Makes Your Trip Smoother 

One of Dubai’s best features is its multicultural nature. More than 200 nationalities call this home and make it a welcoming environment for all who visit. At the same time Dubai is also sensitive about local traditions and customs as well as the respect of culture. Many tourists hope they had an idea of this ahead of time. By dressing right in public, acting right in places of worship and knowing the nodding etiquette of the people you will be interacting with, simple things make interaction a smooth and pleasant one.

Visitors who take the time to understand cultural expectations often find it easier to navigate the city confidently and enjoy a more positive experience overall.

According to Local lifestyle insights often shared by [**Khaleej Times Lifestyle**](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle) highlight how cultural respect enhances the visitor experience in the UAE.

## Peak Tourist Season Changes Everything 

Dubai presents itself as a fantastic winter destination but people don’t know just how sought after Dubai becomes during the winter. The number of visitors surges during the months of November to March. The rooms of the hotels get filled up, the buses book up, the restaurants get booked and the services of transport companies increase. This season brings fabulous weather, but also demands for better planning.

Those who book everything in advance get a much smoother travel experience for their lodging, meals and activities. Some choices might not be available, or delay the expense, at the last minute. This is particularly true for luxury experiences or top notch attractions, desert safaris, yacht charters or fine dining spots.

## Dubai Offers Much More Than Shopping Malls 

One of the most popular Dubai Myths is that of only shopping and luxury.Shopping and luxury is the one thing everyone thinks about when visiting Dubai. The city is, of course, great at both, but there’s so much more that is hidden underneath.

The most interesting things to find outside the malls are what the visitors get to see.

Dubai’s historic neighborhoods showcase its rich heritage. Traditional souks are a display of local culture and craftsmanship. Adventures to see in the desert landscapes. Along the waterfront are waterfront promenades with fabulous views. The city’s identity is enhanced by art districts, museums, cultural centers, and architectural landmarks.

## Small Costs Add Up Faster Than Expected 

Although Dubai has something for everyone to enjoy, visitors tend to underestimate how quickly day to day expenses can add up. Luxury travel packages, meals, entertainment, tickets to attractions, an extra luxury experience and free spontaneous purchases may cost your holiday more than you’d like to think. The city offers both cheap and fancy options; this is why it attracts a variety of tourists. It is a good idea to plan an approximate daily expenditure before you come. Knowing the cost upfront will save the travellers from making any unexpected discoveries and enable them to enjoy their trip instead of worrying about the cost.

Often, the best experiences are the ones which take experience and preparation.

## The Best Experiences Often Require Advance Planning 

A lot of visitors think they can just come and make up their minds as they go along, day by day. Traveling spontaneously is great but Dubai loves a well-prepared traveler. Popular experiences, especially in busy seasons, can fill up quickly. Major sights, gourmet restaurants, luxury experiences and events, and seasonal features often demand reservations. This is the situation business travelers have as well. Demand can spike in any city when it comes to conferences, exhibitions, and corporate events. 

The visitors who get the smoothest experience are those who have made a few of the necessities plans before they show up.  Just making small arrangements like making a sightseeing list, making early travel reservations, and finding out about the transportation options can make a good trip great.
