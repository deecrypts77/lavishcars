## Why Age Matters in Car Rentals

Most companies see an individual under the age of 25 as “high risk” due to less driving experience. Passengers in luxury cars coupled with high-performance vehicles only increase exposure risk even further.

Because of this logic, almost all rental services in The UAE:

- Only provide limited car options 
- Charge additional fees like security deposits and higher “young driver fees.” 
- Prevents young adults from renting entirely.

However, the good news is some agencies do make exceptions, especially if specific required conditions are met. Let’s take a look at such options.

## What’s the Legal Driving Age in Dubai?

To drive and hire a car in Dubai:

- Age requirement: 21 years old
- Some supercars: only 25+

If you live there and own the car, you can legally drive from the age of 18, but you have to be at least 21 to rent one.

## Common Fees for Under-25 Rentals

If you’re 21-24 years old, look what’s in store for you:

- **Young Driver Fee**

This applies to younger clientele as an added insurance fee because of perceived higher risk. AED 100-200 per day (varies by company and car)

- **Higher Security Deposit**

Luxury rentals require a security deposit, and for those under 25, this can often be increased.

**Standard deposit:** AED 5,000 Under 25 deposit: AED 7,000-10,000

- **Limited Vehicle Options**

Certain high-end vehicles, such as the [Ferrari F8 Tributo](/uae/dubai#fleet) or [Lamborghini Huracan](/uae/dubai#fleet) and even [McLaren 720S](/uae/dubai#fleet) are not offered to drivers under the age of twenty-five. However, accessible cars include the Mercedes G63 and Range Rover, along with some Bentley models.

## What Documents Do You Need?

Here is what you’ll need if you’re under 25 and hiring a car in Dubai:

**Tourists:**

- A valid driver’s license from an authorized nation is required.
- [Permit to Drive Abroad](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/passports-and-traveling/driving-abroad) (if required)
- Visa entrance stamp plus passport
- (For security deposit) Credit card

**Residents:&#xA0;**

- a driver’s license from the UAE
- Emirates ID
- A copy of your passport
- A credit card

## How to Increase Your Chances of Getting Approved (Under 25)

Dream car rental restrictions for those under twenty-five can be relaxed with these tips:

1. **Clean Driving Record**

No fines, violations, or accidents on record help your case immensely. This is information some rental agencies may look into.

2. **Full Coverage Insurance**

Show commitment by asking for full coverage insurance, which demonstrates responsibility.

3. **Luxury Hotel Booking or Business Travel Proof**

When you are working on a 5-star hotel or going out on business, this helps to gain credibility.

4. **Make a Larger Down-Payment**

Depositing a little more is going to be responsible and hold trust.

5. **Chauffered Rent**

Suppose a car cannot be self-driven under 25; you can hire it with a driver. This is applicable to Rolls Royce, Bentley, and other luxurious vehicles.
