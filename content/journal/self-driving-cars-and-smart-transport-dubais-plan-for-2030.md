## Dubai’s Vision: Self-Driving Mobility by 2030

Dubai Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) set the target of having 25 percent of all journeys driven by a vehicle free of a driver by 2030. This includes:

- Self-driving cabs drifting across an area of Dubai Marina
- Self-driving buses and shuttles to Expo City Dubai.
- Rentals using AI as a core component letting a tourist have an automated driving experience.

The Smart Dubai 2030 strategy is not only about conveniences there is a purpose behind it, and the purpose is to reduce congestion, reduce emissions, and to make Dubai one of the urban mobility innovators on the planet.

_Did you know? Dubai tested Tesla Model X SUVs as one of the earlier cities to pilot autonomous vehicles as part of its RTA fleet._

## The Future of Rentals: From Chauffeured Luxury to AI Chauffeurs

Dubai has long been sustained by two types of luxury rental.

- Self-Drive Rentals: Customers get an experience of the feeling of driving a Lamborghini Huracan or a Bentley Continental along the Sheikh Zayed Road.
- Chauffeur-driven Rentals- Ideal situations include business executives or vacationers who would feel more comfortable with a Rolls Royce and a chauffeur.

There is now a third model beginning to appear:

- Autonomous Rentals -The car becomes the chauffeur

## Key Technologies Driving Smarter Mobility

The autonomous rentals in Dubai are run with the combination of a new generation of technologies:

- AI, machine learning- Cars can learn traffic patterns and make on-demand decisions.
- LiDAR & Cameras – full visibility of pedestrians, lights and obstructions.
- 5G Connectivity – Communication among vehicles and the smart road network in Dubai will be instant.
- Cloud Integration – Rentals have the option of syncing their apps, thus enabling complete customization of routes, stops and entertainment.

## Costs: Will Autonomous Rentals Be Expensive?

The prices of luxurious cars rent in Dubai are within the range of AED 1,500/day to AED 4,500+/day depending on the type of the car.

### How will autonomy affect prices?

- Tax Saving to Business Travellers – No chauffeur costs.
- Dynamic Pricing – AI-based charges based on traffic, routes, and demand.
- Subscription Models- Monthly pay to access to a fleet of self-driving luxury cars.

Example:

A Bentley Flying Spur with a driver would cost AED 3,000/day, but with AI a self-driving model could cost AED 2,400/day- same luxury but now with intelligence behind the wheel.

## Challenges Dubai Must Solve

There are challenges along the way towards 2030:

- Legal Languages: Who is liable in case of an accident?
- Crossing Borders: Will you be able to drive an autonomous car across the border into Saudi Arabia?
- Public Confidence: Will the tourists have more confidence in the AI than the human chauffeurs?
- Luxury Brand Adaptation: Not every car manufacturer is ready to entrust AI with command.

## Autonomous Rentals Beyond Dubai

Not only will autonomous rentals drive the transformation of mobility in Dubai, but it will also transform cross-border travel:

- Dubai to Saudi Arabia – Picture flawless AI navigation at Al Batha, border, with your documents already verified, uploaded online.
- Tourism Circuits There are also Dubai to Abu Dhabi, to Al Ain, and Ras Al Khaimah handled through self-driving fleets.
- Smart Tourism Packages – Make a reservation in a Tesla robo-rental and add dessert stops in the safari, as well as hotel drop-offs.

## Checklist for Future Autonomous Renters

These are the documents you are most likely to require once you book autonomous rentals in 2030:

- A valid driver permitted (in case of emergency).
- An account with a rental app coupled with an Emirates ID or passport.
- Digital insurance cover (AI-managed).
- A method of payment of dynamic tolls and on-demand.
- Familiarity with technology- cars will not run on keys but on applications.
