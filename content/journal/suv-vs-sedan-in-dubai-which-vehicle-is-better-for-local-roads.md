One of the best roads in the world is in Dubai. Roads are wide, streets are smooth, and there are clear road signs which facilitate both locals and visitors to drive safely. As long as you’re renting a car, one question that tends to come to mind is:

**Which one is better, an SUV or a sedan?**

It depends on what you want to do with your car. A family visiting Dubai might require greater space for bags and kids. For a businessman, a sleek sedan for business trips could be a great choice. Someone planning a road trip to Abu Dhabi or Hatta may enjoy the extra comfort of an SUV. Both these types of vehicles are effective on Dubai’s roads, but each type is different. This guide helps you understand the differences in simple terms to help you choose the car that best fits your needs and your pocket.

## What Is the Difference Between an SUV and a Sedan?

### What Is an SUV?

SUV means Sport Utility Vehicle. It can be higher off the ground than a sedan. It features a larger body, more headroom and a larger luggage compartment. Most SUVs are designed to seat between 5 and 7 people. Many people favor SUVs for the fact that they make them feel huge, and they offer a raised outlook of the road.

Some of the most popular SUVs in Dubai include the following:

- Nissan Patrol
- Toyota Land Cruiser
- Toyota Fortuner
- Hyundai Tucson
- Kia Sportage

These cars are often selected by families, friends, or travelers with lots of luggage.

### What Is a Sedan?

Sedans are conventional, four door passenger vehicles with a separate luggage section. Sedans are lower to the ground, and are typically lighter than SUVs. These are easier to operate, park and they don’t always use as much fuel.

Popular sedans in Dubai include the following:

- Toyota Camry
- Honda Accord
- Nissan Altima
- Hyundai Sonata
- Mercedes-Benz C-Class

Sedans are often used for business travel, couples, solo travel and for everyday driving in the city.

## Why Dubai’s Roads Affect Your Choice

The good news is that Dubai roads are designed to support nearly all types of cars. An SUV isn’t always required to be able to drive around the town. Wide, well maintained roads connect most tourist attractions, shopping malls, hotels, beaches and business districts. A sedan vehicle can easily be used for daily commutes around Dubai city’s popular areas, such as Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, and Jumeirah Beach Residence.

If you need more space or are traveling longer distances, an SUV can be more convenient. As an example, if you are traveling with four people and have huge suitcases from Dubai International Airport to your hotel, a SUV will feel more comfortable than a compact sedan. The additional cabin space also makes longer journeys more comfortable when traveling from one city to another.

## Which Vehicle Fits Your Travel Style in Dubai? 

### For Solo Travelers

When traveling alone to Dubai, then a sedan might be a more sensible choice. It can be easily driven on crowded streets, park without much trouble, and also rents for less money. You don’t always need all of the extra room of an SUV as one person typically has just a few bags. You can enjoy the tour of the city in a comfortable sedan and save on fuel costs.

### For Couples

Couples exploring Dubai usually find a sedan more than enough. It features a good ride, comfortable seats and ample storage for shopping bags or luggage. In Dubai, a sedan car makes you feel like you are on a dream run. A high end sedan also adds a touch of style if the trip involves luxurious hotels and fine dining.

### For Families

It can be better for families to have an SUV. Family holidays typically involve additional suitcases, strollers, shopping bags and travel items to fit into, and children require extra space for ease and comfort. Long drives are less tiring, there is more space for storage, and larger seats onboard an SUV. A higher seating position is also beneficial for parents, providing them visibility of traffic.

### For Groups of Friends

It’s so much easier to get around with friends in an SUV. Renting two smaller cars could be a problem; one spacious SUV can easily accommodate everyone along with the bags. This also adds to the fun when you’re travelling in groups to places such as Global Village, Dubai Parks and Resorts or Miracle Garden.

### For Business Travelers

Sedan is the car of choice for business travelers. A sedan is a professional looking vehicle that is comfortable for meetings around the city and provides a smooth ride along Dubai’s highways. Luxury sedans are particularly favored for their ease of use as well as their stately look, such as for airport pick up, corporate events, and executive traveling.

The [**Roads & Transport Authority (RTA Dubai)&#x20;**](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home)states that if you know your route and road conditions, you will know what type of car is best suited for the most cost effective and safe route.

## Fuel Economy: Which Vehicle Saves More?

Fuel may not be the most significant part of your Dubai holiday budget, but it should be considered. Generally speaking, sedans are more fuel-efficient than SUVs are. This is because sedans are lighter and more aerodynamic. Have reduced energy requirements during transportation, particularly in urban traffic. A sedan will help you save on gasoline costs if you expect to spend a lot of time in Dubai sightseeing.

The SUVs take a lot more fuel due to their higher weight and size. This isn’t the case today though as modern SUVs are far more fuel efficient than the early ones and are suited for families and travellers who care more about space than fuel consumption. If you are traveling for a short vacation, the fuel savings may be minimal compared to the extra comfort an SUV can offer.

## Parking and Driving in Dubai: Which Is Easier?

Dubai is known for its wide roads, modern highways, and well-planned streets. Despite this, it may sometimes be hard to find a spot in high traffic areas such as Deira, City Walk, Dubai Marina, and Downtown Dubai. Here the sedan stands out as the better choice. It is smaller and low to the ground, so it is easier to park in tight places. A sedan can make it easier to get around the city if you are spending a large proportion of the day in it for shopping, dining, etc. It is easy to drive an SUV in Dubai because the city has wide roads and ample parking space. Most of the contemporary SUVs also have parking sensors, rear view camera and 360-degree camera which makes parking drastically easier.

## Safety: Are SUVs Safer Than Sedans?

All the rental SUVs and sedans in Dubai are equipped with contemporary safety features.

Most cars for rent offer:

- Multiple airbags
- Anti-lock Braking System (ABS)
- Electronic Stability Control (ESC)
- Lane Departure Warning
- Blind Spot Monitoring
- Adaptive Cruise Control
- Rear-view cameras
- Parking sensors

The driving position of an SUV is higher, offering you a better view down the road. This can aid drivers to better see traffic and road conditions. The sedan will have a lower centre of gravity, however. This can be useful when making turns or changing lanes at greater speeds, creating more stability. Both vehicles are actually quite safe, when operated carefully and maintained well.

## Conclusion

The choice of SUVs or sedans are great options to drive around Dubai. Either option is comfortable and convenient as is the city’s modern road, wide highways and organised infrastructure. A sedan works best if you need to drive a car around town or locally for either work or play in an environmentally friendly and convenient way. An SUV provides additional space, improved road visibility and comfort for families, social groups and longer trips in the UAE. Before [**booking your rental car**](/), think about how many people are travelling with you, how much luggage you’ll carry, and the places you plan to visit. Choosing the right car for your travel will make every mile of Dubai’s roads fun.
