The most notable feature of BYD is its Blade Battery, the innovation that makes EVs safer, longer lived, and more energetic. These batteries are less likely to overheat and they charge under an hour and require fewer replacements in comparison to most of the conventional lithium-ion cells.

The BYD Han EV, to give just one example, boasts a range of up to 521km on a full charge, washes clothes, feeds the children, pays the bills on a full charge, most enough to drive to Abu Dhabi and back, twice! And, as a growing number of charging infrastructures such as the DEWA Green Charger network and the malls, hotels, and tourist hotspots become fast chargers, it is simple and easy to charge your BYD.
