## The Battle of Luxury SUVs: Cullinan vs Urus

Before we explore celebrity preferences, it’s important to understand what each vehicle brings to the table.

![Lamborghini Urus - The Raging Bull](/images/journal/2025-09-2-9.webp)

### Lamborghini Urus – The Raging Bull

The Lamborghini Urus changed the game when it entered the SUV market. This has been marketed as the first-ever Super “SUV” with an insane Italian design and performance. The Urus boasts aggressive styling, a twin-turbo V8 engine that is powerful and capable of spectacularly quick acceleration ([0 to 100km/h in just 3.6 seconds](https://autobuzz.my/2017/12/05/lamborghini-urus-worlds-first-super-suv-0-100km-h-3-6-seconds/)), and it is impossible to overlook in the streets of Dubai. It is confident, excessive, and constructed with a drive of people who need to be noticeable among the crowd.

![Rolls-Royce Cullinan - The Name of Elegance](/images/journal/2025-09-1-9.webp)

### Rolls-Royce Cullinan – The Name of Elegance

On the other hand, the Rolls-Royce Cullinan is pure luxury on wheels. Named after the largest diamond ever discovered, this British SUV offers an unmatched combination of comfort, elegance, and quiet power. The V12 engine provides a smooth, commanding drive. It doesn’t scream for attention; it quietly demands it. The Cullinan turns heads, not because it tries to, but because it simply exudes class.

## Why Celebrities in Dubai Prefer the Cullinan?

Dubai is a magnet for celebrities, from international artists and film stars to footballers and social media influencers. While many love flashy rides like the Urus, a significant number prefer the Cullinan for the following reasons:

### 1. Comfort and Luxury

Celebrities are used to the finest things in life. The Cullinan offers an experience that goes beyond leather seats and wood trims. Its cabin feels like a five-star suite, with rear massage seats, a starlight headliner, and custom embroidery.

In contrast, the Urus is more performance-focused. While it does offer luxury, it leans more on sportiness and aggressive styling. Celebs looking for peace and pampering often choose the Cullinan.

### 2. A Symbol of Status and Elegance

The Cullinan is not just a car; it’s a statement of high status. Celebrities who want to maintain a sophisticated and polished image find the Cullinan a perfect match. It whispers success, unlike the Urus, which shouts it.

In Dubai, where impressions matter and branding is everything, the Cullinan fits red carpets, luxury hotels, and exclusive private events.

### 3. Privacy and Discretion

Dubai is a hotspot for paparazzi and fans. Celebrities who value privacy love the Cullinan’s tinted windows, spacious rear seat setup, and ultra-quiet cabin. It acts like a moving safe space where they can relax and escape the public eye.

The Urus, being flashy and loud, often invites attention. While that works for public events, it may not be ideal for downtime or off-the-grid luxury.

### 4. Customizations Galore

Rolls-Royce is known for allowing extreme levels of customization. From monogrammed headrests to personalized color schemes and even champagne coolers in the rear seats, celebrities love how the Cullinan becomes an extension of their personality.

Although Lamborghini offers customization too, Rolls-Royce takes it a step further. For A-listers, uniqueness matters, and the Cullinan offers it in abundance.

### 5. Smooth Drive for Dubai Roads

Dubai’s road infrastructure is excellent, but city traffic, long desert drives, and long-haul trips require comfort. The Cullinan’s “Magic Carpet Ride” suspension provides a ride so smooth that passengers barely feel bumps or curves.

For celebs who have hectic schedules, attending events or shooting scenes across the city, a relaxed drive makes a huge difference. The Urus is thrilling to drive, but not as soothing as the Cullinan.
