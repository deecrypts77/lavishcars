## 1. A Building Block of History: Chevrolet and its amazing History

Chevrolet did not transform into a global giant in one day. Its reliability is based on years of engineering reliability, technological progress and customer-first ethos. Performance and Practicality are the foundations of this company.  

The mission of the company was evident when Louis Chevrolet and William C. Durant established the company in 1911:  

- Design low cost cars, targeting common people.  
- Provide performance that buyers desire.  
- Build cars that last  

This is the philosophy of Chevrolet nowadays. The models such as the Suburban, Camaro and Corvette were well known all around the world due to their appearance, dependability, and value in the long run. Those reliability have brought Chevrolet the loyalty of millions of people all over the world.
