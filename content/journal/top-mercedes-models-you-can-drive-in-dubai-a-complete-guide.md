## How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Mercedes in Dubai?

The cost of renting a Mercedes depends on the model, time and season.

Here’s a quick overview:

– Economy Models (A-Class or C-Class): AED 400-900/day.

– Mid-End Models (E-Class, GLC): AED 800-1500/day.

– Luxury Models (S-Class, G63 AMG): AED 1800-3000/day.

– Ultra-Luxury (Maybach, AMG GT): AED 3,000 to 4,500/day.

## Tips Before You Rent a Mercedes in Dubai

- Check License Requirement: Visitors have to have an international driving permit on top of their home country license.
- Check Insurance Protection: This is to ensure that you know what would be covered, collision damage waiver, theft protection, etc.
- Check the Vehicle: Get a snap or video shot in the lot.
- Inquire about Mileage restrictions: certain rentals incorporate the number of kilometers per day, they should be clarified in advance.
- Book Ahead during Peak Season: Only during winter and holiday periods do we experience demand spikes.
