Lamborghini is one of the brands that bring passion when it comes to luxury supercars. These cars have been known to have roaring engines, sharp designs and pure Italian craftsmanship but these are not just cars but rolling works of art. Be it riding along the Dubai Downtown or coming into the Palm Jumeirah in a Lamborghini, one car invariably catches an eye.

However, behind that authority and status, a captivating tale of daring risks, surprising roots, and miracle engineering workings is present. So, it is time to immerse yourself in 10 facts that you might have not known, and each of them tells you why this iconic brand will stay with car lovers all over the world.

![Green Lambo](/images/journal/2025-07-2-7.webp)

## 1. Lamborghini is an establishment that emerged as a result of a dispute between Lamborghini and Ferrari

Ferruccio Lamborghini, the manufacturer of a tractor company, and a Ferrari driver, was not satisfied with the reliability of his vehicles. He informed Enzo Ferrari that the clutch was not okay and he told him to remain with tractors. This prompted Ferruccio to develop Automobili Lamborghini in 1963 to come up with a better grand tourer. What started as revenge turned out to become one of the legendary car competitions of all time.

## 2. The company started with the Lamborghini 350 GT

Lamborghini released it in 1964, which is its first car. It had a 3.5-liter V12 which was a ground breaking engine at the time and offered 280 hp. The 350 GT was luxurious as well as fast, and it immediately put Lamborghini in the upper-cut of competition with Ferrari.

## 3. Lamborghini Designs cars based on the fighting bulls

Most Lamborghini models are named after the savage bulls. Born in the Taurus sign, [Ferruccio Lamborghini](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history) liked the Spanish fighting bulls. Gallo, Murcielo, Diablo and Aventador models indicate this tradition, which denotes power, aggression and luxury.

## 4. On one occasion Lamborghini made a V12 engine on a boat

It is not only that Lamborghini manufactured cars but also a 9.3 liter V12 engine that powered the luxury speedboat, Riva Aquarama. That motor demonstrated the DNA of the brand on water, and the Aquarama was one of the fastest water boats of that time and the testament that Lamborghini is not only an innovator on the road but also on the water.

## 5. The predecessor of the current supercar was the Lamborghini Miura.

In 1966 Lamborghini released Miura, the first mid-engine motorcar in the world to be offered in a road version. The majority of sports cars that existed were front-engined. The Miura with its smooth shapes and innovative design served as a prototype of all new supercars and the models such as Ferrari, McLaren and Porsche.

## 6. Lamborghini Almost Staggered to the Brink of Bankruptcy Three Times

Although Lamborghini is a prestigious company nowadays, it has been through hard times. The oil crisis of 1973 really struck the market of super cars and Ferruccio sold the company. Over time, the brand was transferred to Chrysler and then to the Indonesian investors and eventually to Audi ( Volkswagen group) in 1998. Lamborghini became steady under Audi and is now a global brand with its icons like the Aventador, Huracan and Urus.

## 7. Lamborghini Urus is The Fastest SUV in the World

The fact that Lamborghini launched the Urus in 2018 made numerous individuals question whether a so-called super-SUV might help the brand to maintain its position. They were wrong. The Urus also runs upon a 4.0-liter V8 twin-turbo that generates 650 horsepower. It is able to achieve 305km/h and it is amongst the quickest SUVs to have been constructed.  

The Urus has a combination of performance on the track and comfort. It is popular among the [Lamborghini car hires in Dubai](/uae/dubai#fleet) as people desire to have speed and spaciousness.

## 8. Lamborghini is based on Jet fighter design  

Ever wonder why Lamborghinis are fighter jets? The designers intentionally recreate the features of fighter jets. These are sharp edges, aggressive lines, and cockpit-like interiors.  

The philosophy is reflected on cars such as the Aventador and Reventon where the drivers get the impression of driving a jet rather than a car.

## 9. The Headquarters of Lamborghini is still in Sant’Agata Bolognese

Even though Lamborghini is a global brand, it is still strongly attached to Italy. It has headquarters and a factory in Sant’Agata Bolognese, whose Lamborghini Ferruccio started his dream.  

All their cars are made in Italy, maintaining the old Italian craftsmanship and employing the latest technology in order to produce the modern pieces of art.

## 10. Lamborghini Produces some of the rare cars in the world

Lamborghini is a company that embraces exclusivity. Such models as the Veneno, Centenario, and Sián FKP 37 are available in extremely small quantities – sometimes as few as a handful in the world.  

As an example, there were only nine Veneno Roadsters manufactured, and sold at a price of more than 4m each. These hypercars turn out as the fantasies of the collector and are characterized by the fact that Lamborghini is obsessed with the idea of limit pushing.

## Bonus Fact Lamborghini Doesn’t Compromise in the Engine

In contrast to most companies that manufacture cars by outsourcing engines or slowing down performance due to regulations, Lamborghini remains loyal to its very core. Each car is designed with unbiased power, feeling, and design, and thus making every ride both a tactile and emotional experience.  

Be it the thunderous sound of a V12 Aventador or the humming of the hybrid Sián in the future, a Lamborghini will never be satisfied with the usual.
